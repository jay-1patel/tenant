# DLF Commercial: Offices and IT Parks

## Rental arm
- Held in **DLF Cyber City Developers Ltd (DCCDL)**, a JV between DLF (about 67%) and GIC (Singapore sovereign fund).
- Operational portfolio about 40.4 msf (36.4 msf office, 4 msf retail) as of early 2024.
- DLF's Gurugram offices: about **24.5 msf operational and 10 msf under construction** (per dlf.in).

## DLF Cyber City, Gurugram
- Described by DLF as India's largest integrated business district and first Knowledge City.
- Includes Rapid Metro and dedicated road networks developed by DLF.
- Early tenants included GE, American Express, IBM, British Airways, Nestlé.

## DLF office properties in Gurugram (per dlf.in)
DLF Cyber City, Atrium Place, DLF Downtown, DLF Cyberpark, DLF Horizon Center, DLF World Techpark, DLF Galaxy Tower, DLF Square, DLF Atria, DLF Nestlé House, DLF Centre Court, DLF Plaza Tower.

## Key projects
- **DLF Cyber Park (on NH-48, Gurugram):** 2.5 msf on 12 acres, three towers, LEED Platinum, pre-leased almost fully to clients such as Bank of America, Gartner, and WeWork.
- **DLF Downtown, Gurugram:** new phase of about 5.5 msf Grade A+ offices under construction (part of ~₹6,000 crore plan with Mall of India).
- **DLF Downtown, Chennai:** IT park with value-added retail.
- **DLF Cybercity Chennai** and **The Hub, Chennai:** existing IT/office assets.
- **DLF Midtown (Delhi):** DLF Towers offices as part of ONE Midtown.
