## Complaint escalation ladder
1. **Level 1:** Contact customer care (helpline, app, email, or branch). Resolution target: within 5-7 working days. [DEMO]
2. **Level 2:** Escalate to the Nodal Officer / Grievance Redressal Officer if unresolved in 7 days or if you are unsatisfied.
3. **Level 3:** Escalate to the Principal Nodal Officer.
4. **RBI Ombudsman:** If the bank doesn't respond within 30 days, or you are unsatisfied, file a complaint under the RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme at cms.rbi.org.in. This is free and can be done online.

## What the bot should capture for a complaint
Name, registered mobile number, account/loan/card last 4 digits (never full numbers), date and nature of the issue, and complaint reference if any. Hand off to a human.
