# NRI Investments and Legal FAQs

Disclaimer: general information, not legal or tax advice. Rules change; verify with a CA/lawyer or the RBI. **[CONFIRM]**

## Can NRIs and OCIs buy DLF property?
- Yes. Under FEMA, NRIs and OCI cardholders can buy residential and commercial property in India without prior RBI approval.
- **Not permitted:** agricultural land, plantation property, and farmhouses.

## Payments
- Through normal banking channels: inward remittance, or NRE, NRO, or FCNR(B) accounts. Payments in rupees. No cash. Informal channels (such as hawala) are illegal.
- Keep all remittance proofs; they matter for repatriation later.

## Remote purchase
- Possible via a registered and properly attested **Power of Attorney** granted to a trusted person in India. Beware of frauds; verify project and promoter name and RERA validity.

## Tax points (general)
- **Buying from a resident seller:** the buyer deducts **1% TDS** where consideration exceeds ₹50 lakh (Section 194IA). For FY2025-26 pay via Form 26QB; for FY2026-27 onward the process uses Form 141, within 30 days of each payment. **[CONFIRM current form/process]**
- **Buying from a developer** is generally a primary purchase; TDS applies per the transaction. **[CONFIRM with CA]**
- **When an NRI sells property,** the buyer deducts TDS at higher rates; capital gains rules apply. Repatriation is subject to FEMA limits.

## Home loans for NRIs
- Available from Indian banks; funds are typically disbursed to the developer's account per schedule. Documents: passport, visa/OCI, income proof, bank statements, PoA if applicable.

## Legal FAQs
**Q: What is RERA?** Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016. Developers must register projects; buyers should verify registration number and details before paying.
**Q: Where do I check RERA status?** Haryana RERA (HRERA) website for Gurugram; Delhi RERA for Delhi projects.
**Q: Who is the promoter for DLF luxury projects?** Often DLF Home Developers Limited (for example, The Arbour, Privana). Check the project's RERA page.
**Q: How do I avoid fraud?** Use only dlf.in, pay only to the developer's official account named in the agreement, never pay cash, and check RERA.
