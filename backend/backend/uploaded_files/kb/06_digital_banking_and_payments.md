# Digital Banking and Payments

## Channels
- **Meridian Mobile app** (Android/iOS), **Net Banking**, **WhatsApp banking** [DEMO number: use official number from the app], **ATMs**, **phone banking (24x7)**.
- Register with your Aadhaar/PAN-linked mobile number; set up a login PIN and enable biometrics.

## Fund transfer options (general rules)
| Mode | Availability | Typical limits |
|---|---|---|
| UPI | 24x7 | Typically ₹1 lakh per transaction (higher for some categories) |
| IMPS | 24x7 | Up to ₹5 lakh |
| NEFT | 24x7 | No minimum or maximum (bank may set limits) |
| RTGS | 24x7 | Minimum ₹2 lakh |

- Bank-specific daily limits and charges are set in the app. [DEMO]
- **Failed transaction but money debited?** Usually auto-reversed within a few working days. Contact the bank if not reversed in 5 working days.

## Safe banking tips
- Use only the official app from the App Store/Google Play.
- Never share OTP, PIN, CVV, or passwords; Meridian Bank will never ask.
- Do not click links in unknown SMS or WhatsApp messages; do not install screen-sharing apps at a caller's request.
- Check the UPI collect request carefully: you never need to enter your PIN to receive money.

## Common questions
**Q: How do I register for net banking?** Use "Register" on the net banking page with your customer ID or card details and OTP, or visit a branch.
**Q: I forgot my UPI PIN.** Reset it in the app with your debit card details and OTP.
**Q: My transaction failed.** Wait for the automatic reversal; if not credited in 5 working days, raise a complaint. [DEMO]
