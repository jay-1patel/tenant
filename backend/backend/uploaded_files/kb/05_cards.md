# Debit and Credit Cards (DEMO)

## Debit cards
- Classic, Platinum, and Signature variants; contactless and chip-enabled.
- Daily limits (default) [DEMO]: ATM withdrawal ₹40,000 (Classic) to ₹1,00,000 (Signature); POS/online ₹1,00,000 to ₹3,00,000. Limits are adjustable in the app.
- Domestic, international, and online usage can be switched on/off in the app.

- **Interest-free period:** up to 50 days on purchases if the full bill is paid by the due date.
- **Interest on revolving balance:** around 3.5% per month (about 42% p.a.). [DEMO]
- **Minimum due:** typically 5% of the bill. Paying only the minimum leads to interest and fees.
- **Eligibility:** age 21-60, steady income, good credit score.

## Lost or stolen card
1. Block it at once in the app, by SMS, or by calling the 24x7 helpline.
2. Report unauthorised transactions promptly; the sooner you report, the lower your liability under RBI rules (zero liability when reported within three working days for third-party breaches).
3. Request a replacement card.

## Common questions
**Q: How do I increase my card limit?** Request in the app; subject to credit review.
**Q: How do I convert a purchase to EMI?** Use the app or call the helpline; a processing fee and interest apply. [DEMO]
**Q: Are there foreign transaction fees?** Yes, a markup of about 3.5% applies on international spends. [DEMO]
