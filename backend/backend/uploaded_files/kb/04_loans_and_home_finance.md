# Loans and Home Finance (DEMO rates and terms)

## Home loan [DEMO]
- **Interest rate:** from 8.50% p.a. (floating, linked to the external benchmark/repo rate). Rate depends on credit score, loan amount, and profile.
- **Loan amount:** ₹10 lakh to ₹10 crore. **Tenure:** up to 30 years.
- **Loan-to-value (LTV):** up to 90% for loans up to ₹30 lakh, up to 80% for ₹30-75 lakh, up to 75% above ₹75 lakh (following RBI norms).
- **Processing fee:** up to 0.50% of the loan (min ₹5,000, max ₹25,000) + GST.
- **Prepayment:** no foreclosure charge on floating-rate loans for individuals (RBI rule).
- **Eligibility (typical):** age 21-65 at maturity; salaried (2+ years of experience) or self-employed (3+ years of business); good credit score (750+ preferred).
- **Documents:** KYC, last 3 months' salary slips, 6 months' bank statements, Form 16/ITR, property documents, allotment letter or builder agreement.
- **Developer tie-ups:** Meridian Bank maintains an approved-projects list for leading developers, which can speed up processing. Check whether a specific project is approved. [DEMO]
- **Pre-approved loan:** apply before you choose a property to know your budget.
- **Balance transfer:** move an existing home loan from another lender; top-up available.
- **NRI home loans:** available (see 07_nri_banking.md).

## EMI formula (general)
EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where P = principal, r = monthly rate, n = number of months.
Example (illustrative): ₹50 lakh at 8.50% for 20 years gives an EMI of about ₹43,391.

## Common questions
**Q: How is my home loan eligibility decided?** Income, existing obligations, credit score, age, property value, and tenure. A human advisor will assess it.
**Q: Can I prepay my loan?** Yes. For individual floating-rate loans, no prepayment penalty applies.
**Q: Do you finance under-construction properties?** Yes, with disbursement linked to construction stages. Check RERA registration. [DEMO]
**Q: What is my credit score?** Credit scores come from credit bureaus (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF). Each individual can get a free annual report.
