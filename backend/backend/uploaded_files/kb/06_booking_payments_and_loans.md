# Booking, Payments, and Home Loans

Note: DLF's exact payment plans, booking amounts, and offers vary by project and time. The steps below are the **general** process. Specific numbers are not stated here on purpose. **[CONFIRM with sales]**

## General booking steps
1. **Enquiry and shortlisting:** choose project, configuration, budget.
2. **Site visit / presentation:** with a DLF advisor.
3. **Verify RERA registration:** project must be registered with Haryana RERA (Gurugram) or Delhi RERA (Delhi) before booking.
4. **Application and booking amount:** pay the booking amount via bank transfer/cheque (no cash).
5. **Allotment and builder-buyer agreement:** review carefully with a lawyer.
6. **Construction-linked or other payment plan:** as per the agreement.
7. **Possession and registration:** stamp duty and registration charges apply as per state rules.

## Payment methods (general)
- Cheque, RTGS/NEFT, demand draft. Cash is not recommended or permitted for property payments.
- NRIs must pay via NRE/NRO/FCNR(B) accounts or inward remittance.

## Home loans
- Many banks list DLF projects as approved (for example, HSBC's approved list includes DLF Privana South, Privana North, The Camellias, The Dahlias, One Midtown, DLF 63). Loan eligibility depends on the applicant. **[CONFIRM approval per bank]**
- Typical documents: ID and address proof, PAN, income proofs, bank statements, allotment letter.
- Home-loan interest rates, tenure, and LTV vary by lender; refer users to the bank.

## Charges to ask about
- Basic sale price, preferential location charges, car parking, club membership, maintenance deposit, GST (as applicable), stamp duty and registration. **[CONFIRM per project]**

## FAQs
**Q: What is the booking amount?** Varies by project; connect to sales.
**Q: Can I get a refund on cancellation?** Depends on the agreement and RERA provisions; escalate to a human.
**Q: Is GST applicable?** Depends on project stage and category; advise consulting the sales team or a CA.
