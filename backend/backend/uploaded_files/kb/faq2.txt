Q: How do I book a tour package?
A: Pick your package or destination, share your travel dates and number of travellers, and our team will send a quotation. Once you approve it, pay the booking amount and we confirm your reservation.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept UPI, bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS), debit and credit cards, and cash at our office. You’ll receive a receipt for every payment.

Q: Can I pay in instalments?
A: Yes. You can pay the booking amount first and the rest in two or three instalments, as long as the full amount is cleared before the final payment date.

Q: Will I get a confirmation after booking?
A: Yes. You’ll receive a booking confirmation with your itinerary, hotel details and inclusions on WhatsApp and email within 24 hours of payment.

Q: Are GST and taxes included in the package price?
A: Package prices shown to you in the final quotation include applicable GST. Overseas tours may attract TCS as per government rules, which will be mentioned in your quote.

Q: Can I customize a package?
A: Yes. We can change hotels, add days, include extra cities, or remove activities. Share your preferences and we’ll prepare a customized quote.

Q: Do you offer group tours or only private tours?
A: Both. We run fixed-departure group tours at lower per-person rates, and private tours for families, couples and small groups who want a flexible schedule.

Q: What are the best months to travel?
A: It depends on the destination. For example, Leh Ladakh is best from May to September, Dubai from November to March, and Europe from April to October. Tell us your destination and we’ll suggest the best time.

Q: Are your tours suitable for senior citizens and kids?
A: Yes. We plan comfortable itineraries with fewer long drives for seniors, and child-friendly activities and family rooms for kids. Please share the ages of your travellers when you request a quote.

Q: Do you arrange vegetarian and Jain food?
A: Yes. Pure vegetarian and Jain meals can be arranged at most destinations. Please tell us your food preference at the time of booking.



Q: Can you book flight tickets only?
A: Yes. We book domestic and international flights separately, with or without a tour package.

Q: Are flights included in your tour packages?
A: Packages are priced land-only by default. We can add flights to your package at an additional cost.

Q: Can I choose my hotel?
A: Yes. Each package has a standard hotel category (3-star, 4-star or 5-star), and you can upgrade or choose a specific hotel for an adjusted price.

Q: Can I get a room with a specific view or bed type?
A: Preferences such as sea view, twin or double beds can be requested, but they are subject to hotel availability at the time of check-in.

Q: What are the check-in and check-out timings?
A: Standard check-in is at 2:00 PM and check-out at 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out depends on availability and may cost extra.



Q: Do you provide visa assistance?
A: Yes. We help with form filling, document checking, appointments and submission for most countries. Final approval is always decided by the embassy.

Q: What documents do I need for an international trip?
A: A passport valid for at least 6 months, passport-size photos, bank statements, ITR, and employment proof. Requirements differ by country, and our team will share the exact list.

Q: Do I need a passport for domestic tours?
A: No, but you need a valid government photo ID such as Aadhaar, voter ID or driving licence. Some areas, such as Lakshadweep and parts of the North East, need permits that we arrange.

Q: Is travel insurance necessary?
A: It’s mandatory for Schengen countries and strongly recommended for every international trip. We can arrange domestic and overseas insurance for you.

Cancellation & Changes

Q: What is your cancellation policy?
A: Cancellation charges depend on how close to departure you cancel. As a general guide: 30+ days before travel, charges are limited to the booking amount; 15–29 days, 50% of the package cost; within 14 days, up to 100%. Flights, visas and cruises follow the airline, embassy or cruise-line rules.

Q: Can I change my travel dates after booking?
A: Yes, subject to availability and any difference in hotel and flight costs. Please inform us at least 15 days before departure.

Q: What happens if my flight gets cancelled or delayed?
A: Contact us right away. We’ll work with the airline to rebook, adjust your hotel and transfers, and help with refund claims where applicable.

Q: What if the weather affects the trip?
A: For weather, road closures or safety reasons, we rearrange sightseeing wherever possible. Refunds for unused services depend on the supplier’s policy.



Q: Will someone receive me at the airport?
A: Yes. Our driver or representative will meet you at the airport or station with a name board. Pickup details are sent a day before arrival.

Q: Is there a 24x7 support number during the trip?
A: Yes. You’ll get an emergency contact number with your itinerary, and you can also message us on WhatsApp anytime.

Q: What should I carry for the trip?
A: Your passport or ID, printed or digital tickets and vouchers, travel insurance copy, medicines, comfortable footwear, weather-appropriate clothes, and a power bank.

Q: Can I extend my stay or add extra nights?
A: Yes, depending on hotel availability. Tell us in advance or during the trip, and we’ll quote the extra cost.



Q: Which cabin should I choose?
A: Interior cabins are the most affordable, ocean-view cabins have a window, and balcony cabins have a private outdoor space. If you want to enjoy the views, ocean-view or balcony is worth it.

Q: Are meals included on cruises?
A: Yes, all main meals and snacks are included. Specialty restaurants, alcohol and soft drinks are usually charged extra.

Q: How early should I reach the port?
A: Reach the port 3 to 4 hours before departure for check-in and security.
