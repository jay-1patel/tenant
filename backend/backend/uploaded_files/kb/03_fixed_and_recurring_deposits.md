# Fixed and Recurring Deposits (DEMO rates)

## Fixed Deposit (FD) rates, per annum [DEMO]
| Tenure | General | Senior citizen |
|---|---|---|
| 7-45 days | 3.50% | 4.00% |
| 46-179 days | 4.75% | 5.25% |
| 180 days to < 1 year | 6.00% | 6.50% |
| 1 year to < 2 years | 6.75% | 7.25% |
| 2 years to < 3 years | 7.00% | 7.50% |
| 3 years to 5 years | 6.90% | 7.40% |
| Tax-saver FD (5 years) | 6.90% | 7.40% |

- **Minimum:** ₹10,000 to open (₹5,000 for Recurring Deposit instalment). [DEMO]
- **Payout options:** cumulative (reinvested), monthly, or quarterly interest payout.
- **Premature withdrawal:** allowed with a penalty of 0.50-1.00% on the applicable rate. [DEMO]
- **Loan against FD:** up to 90% of the deposit value.
- **Nomination:** available on all deposits.

## Recurring Deposit (RD)
- Tenure 6 months to 10 years, monthly instalment from ₹500. [DEMO]
- Rates are similar to FD of equal tenure.

## Tax points (general)
- Interest on deposits is taxable as income. TDS applies when interest exceeds the threshold in a financial year (it differs for senior citizens). Submit Form 15G/15H where eligible. Check current limits with the Income Tax Department or a CA.
- Tax-saver FD (5-year lock-in) may qualify for deduction under the old tax regime (Section 80C, up to ₹1.5 lakh).

## Common questions
**Q: What is the FD rate today?** See the table (demo figures); rates change, so confirm in the app.
**Q: Are FDs safe?** Deposits are insured by DICGC up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank.
**Q: Can I break my FD early?** Yes, with a penalty.
