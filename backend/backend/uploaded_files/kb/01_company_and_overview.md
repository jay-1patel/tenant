# DLF Limited: Company Overview

## Basics
- **Full name:** DLF Limited (originally Delhi Land & Finance).
- **Founded:** 4 July 1946 by Chaudhary Raghvendra Singh.
- **Type:** Publicly listed company (NSE/BSE: DLF). Described by DLF as the largest publicly listed real estate company in India.
- **Headquarters:** New Delhi. Corporate office: DLF Gateway Tower, R Block, DLF City Phase III, Gurugram 122002. Phone (corporate): +91-124-4396000.
- **CIN:** L70101HR1963PLC002484.
- **Official website:** dlf.in (the only official site; beware of look-alikes).
- **Footprint:** Residential, commercial, and retail properties in 15 states and 24 cities (per DLF's website).
- **IPO:** July 2007, about US$2 billion, India's largest IPO at that time.

## Leadership (as publicly reported)
- **Rajiv Singh**, Chairman.
- **Kushal Pal Singh (K.P. Singh)**, Chairman Emeritus. Left the Army in 1960 and joined American Universal Electric, became MD after its 1979 merger with DLF Universal, chairman in 1995, stepped back in 2020.
- **Sriram Khattar**, Vice Chairman and MD, Rental Business (as of 2024 reports).
- **Aakash Ohri**, Group Executive Director and Chief Business Officer (as of 2023 reports). **[CONFIRM current roles]**

## History
- **1946:** Founded; built 22 urban colonies in Delhi, including Model Town, Rajouri Garden, Krishna Nagar, South Extension, Greater Kailash, Kailash Colony, and Hauz Khas.
- **Mid-1970s onward:** Began DLF City in Gurgaon (now Gurugram), acquiring about 3,500 acres and turning a small town into a corporate hub. Early corporate tenants included GE, American Express, British Airways, IBM, and Nestlé.
- **2007:** IPO.
- **2017:** Promoters sold their stake in the rental arm to GIC, forming DLF Cyber City Developers Ltd (DCCDL).
- **2023 onward:** Luxury push in Gurugram (The Arbour, Privana, The Dahlias).

## Business segments
1. **Development (residential):** Luxury high-rises, premium apartments, independent floors, townships. Sold through subsidiaries such as DLF Home Developers Ltd.
2. **Rental (commercial and retail):** Held mainly in **DLF Cyber City Developers Ltd (DCCDL)**, a joint venture with Singapore's sovereign wealth fund GIC. DLF holds about 67%. DCCDL's operational portfolio was about 40.4 million sq ft (36.4 msf office, 4 msf retail) in early 2024.
3. **Retail/Malls:** Mall of India (Noida), DLF Promenade and Emporio (Vasant Kunj), DLF Avenue (Saket), Cyber Hub, plus new neighbourhood plazas.

## Recent financial snapshot (public disclosures)
- **FY25 sales bookings:** ₹21,223 crore (record).
- **FY26 sales bookings:** ₹20,143 crore.
- **FY27 guidance:** about ₹20,000 crore; launch pipeline of roughly ₹60,000 crore across Gurugram, Mumbai, and Goa.
- **Q1 FY27 (Apr-Jun 2026):** net profit ₹794 crore, revenue ₹1,605 crore, new bookings ₹657 crore (low due to deferred launches), net cash ₹15,200 crore.
- **DCCDL Q1 FY27:** revenue ₹1,917 crore, net profit ₹717 crore.

## Common questions
**Q: Who founded DLF?** Chaudhary Raghvendra Singh, in 1946.
**Q: Is DLF listed?** Yes, on NSE and BSE.
**Q: What does DLF stand for?** Delhi Land & Finance.
**Q: Where does DLF operate?** Mainly Delhi-NCR (Gurugram, Delhi), plus Chandigarh/Panchkula region, Chennai, Goa, and other cities.
