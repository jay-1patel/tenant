# KYC, Security, and Fraud Prevention

## KYC
- **Required:** PAN (or Form 60), proof of identity and address (Aadhaar, passport, voter ID, driving licence, etc.), photograph.
- **Re-KYC:** periodic updates are required; the frequency depends on risk category. Submit through the app, branch, or by post.
- **Video KYC:** done with a bank officer over a live video call.

## Common fraud types
- **Phishing and vishing:** fake calls/SMS asking for OTP or card details.
- **UPI collect and QR scams:** scanning a QR code or approving a request to "receive" money actually debits you.
- **SIM swap, remote-access app fraud, fake customer care numbers, fake loan or KYC-update links.**

## If you are a victim
1. Block the card, UPI, and net banking immediately.
2. Call the bank's 24x7 helpline [helpline number].
3. Report to the National Cyber Crime Helpline **1930** and at cybercrime.gov.in.
4. File a complaint at the branch with details and screenshots.
5. Under RBI rules, a customer's liability is limited if the fraud is reported quickly; delays can increase liability.

## Common questions
**Q: Will the bank call and ask for my OTP?** Never.
**Q: How do I verify the official customer-care number?** Check the app, the back of your card, or the bank's website.
**Q: Someone is asking for money using my friend's account.** Verify by calling your friend directly before sending money.
