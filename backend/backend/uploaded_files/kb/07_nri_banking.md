# NRI Banking (general rules + DEMO features)

## Account types (general, under FEMA)
| Account | Currency | Repatriable? | Source of funds |
|---|---|---|---|
| NRE (Non-Resident External) | INR | Yes (principal + interest) | Foreign earnings remitted to India |
| NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) | INR | Limited (up to USD 1 million per financial year under conditions) | Income earned in India (rent, dividends, etc.) |
| FCNR(B) | Foreign currency | Yes | Foreign currency deposits |

- Interest on NRE and FCNR(B) is generally tax-free in India; NRO interest is taxable (TDS applies). Confirm with a CA.
- Who qualifies: NRIs and PIOs/OCIs.

## Features [DEMO]
- Online account opening with video KYC from abroad (select countries).
- Dedicated NRI relationship manager and helpdesk.
- Remittance: wire transfers from abroad; Meridian partners with remittance providers for low-fee transfers.
- **NRI home loans:** for purchase or construction of residential property; documents include passport, visa/OCI, foreign income proof, PoA if applicable. Disbursement goes to the developer per schedule.
- **Power of Attorney** support for property transactions.

## Common questions
**Q: Which account should I pick for rupee savings in India?** NRE for foreign earnings; NRO for Indian income. A relationship manager can advise.
**Q: Can an NRI buy residential property in India?** Yes, under FEMA, using NRE/NRO/FCNR(B) or inward remittance. Agricultural land and farmhouses are excluded.
**Q: Do NRIs pay tax on NRE interest?** Generally no, but verify with a tax professional.
