# Meridian Bank Extended FAQs (DEMO)

**DEMO DATA:** Meridian Bank is fictional. Bank-specific figures are invented and marked [DEMO]. Confirm all real-world rules before live use.

## Accounts

**Q1. Can I open a joint account?**
A. Yes. You can choose operating modes such as either or survivor, or former or survivor. All holders must complete KYC.

**Q2. Can a minor have a savings account?**
A. Yes, with a parent or guardian. Minors aged 10 and above may operate the account independently within limits set by the bank. [DEMO]

**Q3. How do I add a nominee?**
A. In the app under Services, or at a branch with a nomination form. Nomination is recommended for all accounts and deposits.

**Q4. How do I update my address?**
A. Submit a valid address proof in the app (e-KYC) or at a branch.

**Q5. What is a dormant account?**
A. An account with no customer-initiated transactions for 12 months becomes inactive, and for 24 months, dormant. Visit a branch with KYC to reactivate it. [DEMO]

**Q6. How do I close my account?**
A. Clear all dues, hand over unused cheque leaves and cards, and submit a closure request at your home branch. A fee may apply if closed within 12 months of opening. [DEMO]

**Q7. Can I get a salary account without a zero balance?**
A. Yes. The Salary Account needs no minimum balance while the salary is credited regularly. [DEMO]

## Deposits

**Q8. What is the minimum FD amount?**
A. ₹10,000. [DEMO]

**Q9. Can I open an FD online?**
A. Yes, through the app or net banking in a few minutes.

**Q10. What happens to my FD on maturity?**
A. You can choose auto-renewal or credit to your account. If you give no instruction, the amount is credited to your account or renewed per the option chosen at booking. [DEMO]

**Q11. Is TDS deducted on FD interest?**
A. TDS applies if interest in a financial year crosses the threshold set by the Income Tax Department. Submit Form 15G/15H if eligible. Please check current limits with a tax professional.

**Q12. Can I take a loan against my FD?**
A. Yes, up to 90% of the deposit value. [DEMO]

**Q13. Do senior citizens get higher FD rates?**
A. Yes, typically an extra 0.50% over the general rate. [DEMO]

## Home Loans

**Q14. How much home loan can I get?**
A. It depends on your income, existing EMIs, credit score, age, and the property value. A loan advisor will assess your eligibility.

**Q15. What credit score do I need?**
A. A score of 750 or above is preferred for the best rates. Lower scores may still qualify at higher rates. [DEMO]

**Q16. Is the home loan rate fixed or floating?**
A. Floating, linked to an external benchmark. The rate can change when the benchmark changes. [DEMO]

**Q17. Can I apply with a co-applicant?**
A. Yes. A co-applicant, often a spouse or parent, can improve eligibility. Co-owners are generally required as co-applicants.

**Q18. What is a balance transfer?**
A. Moving your existing home loan from another lender to Meridian Bank, usually to get a lower rate. Check the foreclosure terms and processing fee.

**Q19. How do I know if a property is approved by Meridian Bank?**
A. Ask a loan advisor or check the approved-projects list. Approved projects can speed up processing. [DEMO]

**Q20. Do you finance a flat bought from a builder such as DLF?**
A. Yes, subject to the project being approved and meeting legal checks. Please verify RERA registration and ask a loan advisor whether your project is on the approved list. [DEMO]

**Q21. When is the loan disbursed for an under-construction flat?**
A. In stages, linked to construction progress as per the builder's payment schedule, directly to the builder's account.

**Q22. Will I pay EMI during construction?**
A. You pay pre-EMI (interest only on the amount disbursed) until the full loan is disbursed, then full EMI starts. A full-EMI-from-start option may also exist. [DEMO]

**Q23. Can I claim tax benefits on a home loan?**
A. Under the old tax regime, deductions on principal and interest may be available (Sections 80C and 24(b)), within limits. Please consult a CA, as rules change.

**Q24. Does the bank charge for prepayment?**
A. No prepayment charge on floating-rate home loans for individual borrowers (RBI rule).

**Q25. What if I miss an EMI?**
A. Late fees and interest apply, and it can lower your credit score. Contact the bank early if you expect difficulty.

## Cards

**Q26. How long does credit card approval take?**
A. Usually 3 to 7 working days after document verification. [DEMO]

**Q27. How do I pay my credit card bill?**
A. Through the app, net banking, UPI, auto-debit, or at a branch. Pay the full amount by the due date to avoid interest.

**Q28. What is the credit card billing cycle?**
A. Statements are generated monthly, and the due date is typically about 18 days after the statement date. [DEMO]

**Q29. Why was my card transaction declined?**
A. Common reasons are an insufficient limit, international or online usage switched off, a wrong PIN or OTP, or a security block. Check the usage settings in the app.

**Q30. How do I dispute a card transaction?**
A. Raise a dispute in the app or call the helpline, ideally within 30 days of the statement.

## Digital Banking and UPI

**Q31. Can I use UPI without a debit card?**
A. A debit card is generally needed for initial setup, or Aadhaar-based OTP in some cases. [DEMO]

**Q32. What is the daily UPI limit?**
A. Typically up to ₹1 lakh per day for general transfers, with different limits for certain categories. Your exact limit is in the app. [DEMO]

**Q33. How do I change my registered mobile number?**
A. Visit a branch with ID proof, or use the app with e-KYC verification. [DEMO]

**Q34. I sent money to the wrong person.**
A. Contact the bank immediately via the helpline and raise a dispute. Recovery is not guaranteed, because the recipient must consent to return funds.

**Q35. Why is my net banking locked?**
A. Repeated wrong password attempts lock it. Reset the password using your debit card details and OTP, or visit a branch.

## Security and Fraud

**Q36. I got a call saying my account will be blocked unless I update KYC. Is it real?**
A. Treat it as a scam. Meridian Bank does not ask for OTPs, PINs, or passwords by phone. Hang up and call the official helpline.

**Q37. I clicked on a suspicious link. What should I do?**
A. Change your passwords, block your cards and UPI, and call the helpline and 1930 at once.

**Q38. Am I liable for unauthorised transactions?**
A. If you report a third-party fraud within three working days, your liability is generally zero under RBI rules. Reporting later may limit this. Contact the bank immediately.

## NRI

**Q39. Can I open an NRE account from abroad?**
A. Yes, via video KYC in select countries or by sending documents. [DEMO]

**Q40. Can I send money from my NRO account abroad?**
A. Up to USD 1 million per financial year, subject to conditions and tax compliance (Form 15CA/15CB). Consult your advisor.

## Complaints

**Q41. How do I file a complaint?**
A. Call the helpline, use the app, email customer care, or visit a branch. Note your complaint reference number.

**Q42. What if my complaint isn't resolved?**
A. Escalate to the Nodal Officer and then the Principal Nodal Officer. If still unresolved after 30 days, file with the RBI Ombudsman at cms.rbi.org.in.
