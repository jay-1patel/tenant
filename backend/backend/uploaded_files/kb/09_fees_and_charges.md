# Fees and Charges (DEMO schedule)

All fees are subject to GST and may change. Confirm in the app or at a branch.

| Item | Charge [DEMO] |
|---|---|
| AMB non-maintenance (savings) | Up to ₹500 per quarter, or 5% of the shortfall, whichever is lower |
| Debit card annual fee | ₹0 (Basic) to ₹750 (Signature) |
| Free ATM transactions per month | 5 (own ATMs), 3 in metros at other banks |
| Extra ATM transaction | ₹21 per financial transaction |
| Cheque book | 25 leaves free per year; ₹3 per extra leaf |
| NEFT/RTGS (branch) | ₹2.50 to ₹25 depending on amount; free online |
| IMPS (online) | ₹2.50 to ₹15 depending on amount |
| Demand draft | ₹50 per ₹10,000 (min ₹50) |
| Cheque return (insufficient funds) | ₹300 |
| Account statement (duplicate) | ₹100 |
| Home loan processing | Up to 0.50% (min ₹5,000, max ₹25,000) |
| Credit card late payment | ₹100 to ₹1,300 depending on dues |
| Credit card cash advance | 2.5% (min ₹500) |
| Debit card replacement | ₹200 |

## Common questions
**Q: What happens if I don't maintain the minimum balance?** A non-maintenance charge applies. Basic Savings has no minimum balance.
**Q: Is there a fee for UPI?** UPI payments are generally free for customers.
