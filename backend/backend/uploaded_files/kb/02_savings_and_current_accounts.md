# Savings and Current Accounts (DEMO figures)

| Account | Min. balance (AMB) | Interest | Key features |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meridian Basic Savings | ₹0 (zero balance) | 2.75% p.a. | Debit card, UPI, limited free transactions |
| Meridian Savings Plus | ₹10,000 metro / ₹5,000 semi-urban / ₹2,500 rural | 3.00% p.a. | Free debit card, cheque book, passbook |
| Meridian Salary Account | Zero (while salary is credited) | 3.00% p.a. | Premium debit card, free NEFT/RTGS, overdraft option |
| Meridian Senior Citizens | ₹5,000 | 3.25% p.a. | Extra FD rate, priority service |
| Meridian Current Account | ₹25,000 | None | For businesses; free transactions up to a monthly limit |

- Interest is calculated daily on the end-of-day balance and credited quarterly. [DEMO]
- **Non-maintenance of AMB:** a charge applies (see 09_fees_and_charges.md).

## Opening an account
- **Documents:** PAN (or Form 60), Aadhaar or other officially valid document for address, passport photo.
- **Ways to open:** branch visit, or video KYC through the app (resident Indians, 18+).
- Minors: a minor's account is opened with a parent/guardian. Joint accounts available (either or survivor, former or survivor, etc.).

## Common questions
**Q: What is the minimum balance for a savings account?** It depends on the variant; Basic Savings needs none. [DEMO]
**Q: How long does account opening take?** Usually the same day after KYC verification. [DEMO]
**Q: Can I open an account online?** Yes, via video KYC in the app.
**Q: How do I close my account?** Visit your home branch or submit a request through the app; clear dues first. A closure fee may apply within a short period of opening. [DEMO]
