# Meridian Bank FAQs (DEMO)

**Q1. Is Meridian Bank real?** No; it is a fictional demo bank. Replace the demo data before any live use.
**Q2. How do I open a savings account?** Via the app (video KYC) or at a branch, with PAN and an address proof.
**Q3. What is the minimum balance?** It depends on the variant; Basic Savings needs none.
**Q4. What is the FD rate for 1 year?** 6.75% general / 7.25% senior citizen (demo).
**Q5. Are my deposits insured?** Up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank under DICGC.
**Q6. What is your home loan rate?** From 8.50% p.a. floating (demo); the exact rate depends on your profile.
**Q7. Can I get a loan for an under-construction flat?** Yes, with stage-wise disbursement; check RERA registration.
**Q8. How do I prepay a home loan?** Via the app or branch; individual floating-rate loans have no prepayment penalty.
**Q9. What documents do I need for a home loan?** KYC, income proof, bank statements, property documents.
**Q10. How do I block my card?** In the app, by SMS, or by calling the 24x7 helpline.
**Q11. Someone asked for my OTP. What do I do?** Do not share it. Block your accounts and report to 1930.
**Q12. What is the UPI limit?** Typically ₹1 lakh per transaction; your daily limit is in the app.
**Q13. What are NEFT and RTGS timings?** Both run 24x7. RTGS has a minimum of ₹2 lakh.
**Q14. Can NRIs open accounts?** Yes: NRE, NRO, and FCNR(B).
**Q15. How do I raise a complaint?** Contact customer care, then escalate through the nodal officer, then the RBI Ombudsman.
**Q16. Is there a fee for UPI?** Generally no.
**Q17. How do I change my registered mobile number?** Visit a branch with ID proof or use the app with KYC verification. [DEMO]
**Q18. How do I get a statement?** Download it in the app or net banking; a duplicate paper statement carries a fee.
