## About DLF

**Q1. What is DLF?**
A. DLF Limited (originally Delhi Land & Finance) is a listed real estate company founded in 1946. It develops residential, commercial, and retail properties and describes itself as the largest publicly listed real estate company in India.

**Q2. Who founded DLF?**
A. Chaudhary Raghvendra Singh founded DLF on 4 July 1946.

**Q3. Where is DLF headquartered?**
A. DLF is based in New Delhi. Its corporate office is at DLF Gateway Tower, R Block, DLF City Phase III, Gurugram.

**Q4. Is DLF listed on the stock exchange?**
A. Yes, on NSE and BSE. Its IPO in July 2007 was about US$2 billion, India's largest at that time.

**Q5. What is DLF's official website?**
A. dlf.in is the only official website. Please avoid look-alike sites and unofficial agents.

**Q6. In how many cities does DLF operate?**
A. DLF lists properties across 15 states and 24 cities. Its main focus is Delhi-NCR, especially Gurugram and Delhi.

## NRI and Legal

**Q28. Can NRIs buy DLF property?**
A. Yes. NRIs and OCI cardholders can buy residential and commercial property in India under FEMA without prior RBI approval. Agricultural land, plantation property, and farmhouses are not allowed.

**Q29. How should an NRI pay?**
A. Through inward remittance or NRE, NRO, or FCNR(B) accounts, in rupees. Never use cash or informal channels.

**Q30. Can an NRI buy without visiting India?**
A. Often yes, through a registered and attested Power of Attorney given to a trusted person in India. Verify the project, promoter, and RERA registration first.

**Q31. Is TDS applicable on a property purchase?**
A. When buying from a resident seller, the buyer generally deducts 1% TDS if the price exceeds ₹50 lakh. Please confirm the current process with a CA. **[CONFIRM]**

**Q32. What is RERA and why does it matter?**
A. RERA (Real Estate Regulation Act, 2016) requires developers to register projects. Check the registration on Haryana RERA (Gurugram) or Delhi RERA (Delhi) before paying anything.

**Q33. How do I avoid property fraud?**
A. Use only dlf.in, verify RERA, pay only to the official developer account, never pay cash, and have a lawyer review the agreement.
