## The Dahlias
- Ultra-luxury project on Golf Course Road, Sector 54, spread over about 17 acres with 400+ apartments.
- Reported entry pricing from roughly ₹80 crore; about 221 units worth ~₹16,000 crore were booked by the Sept 2025 quarter.
- Clubhouse of about 200,000 sq ft (about twice the size of The Camellias' clubhouse, per press reports).
- Carpet-area realisation reported above ₹1 lakh per sq ft. **[CONFIRM current pricing with sales]**

## The Camellias
- Sector 42, Golf Course Road; about 429 units across 16 towers. LEED Platinum certified (per public listings). **[CONFIRM]**

## The Arbour
- Location: DLF Sixtythree, Sector 63, Golf Course Extension Road. Entry to the Golf Course Extension micro-market for DLF.
- About 25 acres, five towers of 38/39 storeys, 1,137 four-bedroom apartments (with study and utility room).
- Launch pricing was from about ₹7 crore per unit (2023). Today's resale prices differ. **[CONFIRM]**
- Pre-certified IGBC Platinum. DLF said over 95% of buyers were end-users.
- Sold out ₹8,000+ crore in three days before formal launch (March 2023).
- Pipeline reports mention "Arbour 2" and a senior-living offering in FY27. **[CONFIRM]**

## DLF Privana township (Sectors 76-77)
- About 116 acres, integrated township, developed under DLF Home Developers Ltd.
- **Privana West:** 795 units, sold in 3 days in May 2024 for about ₹5,590 crore.
- **Privana South:** Sold out. West and South together: about ₹12,800 crore.
- **Privana North:** 17.7 acres, six towers of stilt + 50 storeys (DLF's tallest residential towers), 1,150+ units, about ₹11,000 crore sold within a week of launch (Q1 FY26). Curvilinear tower placement with views of central greens, the Aravallis, and a 500 m-wide green zone.
- **Connectivity:** Southern Peripheral Road (SPR), NH-48, Dwarka Expressway; near DLF Corporate Greens, TCS, and the American Express campus.

## ONE Midtown (Delhi)
- Luxury residential ecosystem at Moti Nagar: residences, a residents-only clubhouse, Midtown Community Center (healthcare, retail, banquets), **Midtown Plaza** (retail, now operational), and DLF Towers (offices).
