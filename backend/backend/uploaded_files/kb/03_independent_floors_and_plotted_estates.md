# Independent Floors, Townships, and Other Residential

Data freshness: general public information; details are limited. Treat as orientation only and **[CONFIRM]** with sales.

## Independent floors
- DLF has offered both apartments and independent floors at different price points (per management comments).
- **DLF Gardencity (Sectors 91/92, Gurugram):** reported to offer 2, 3, and 4 BHK apartments and independent floors. **[CONFIRM]**
- **Hamilton Court / Hamilton II:** appears in FY27 launch pipeline reports with ~₹8,000 crore GDV. **[CONFIRM scope and type]**
- Independent floors typically mean low-rise (stilt + 3-4 floors) homes on a plot, each floor sold as an independent unit with its own entry, usually with a parking space.
