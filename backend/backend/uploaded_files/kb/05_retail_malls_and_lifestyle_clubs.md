# DLF Retail, Malls, and Lifestyle

## Operating and planned destinations
| Property | Location | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| DLF Mall of India | Noida | Existing large format mall |
| DLF Mall of India, Gurugram | Gurugram | About 2 msf; under construction (started FY24) |
| DLF Promenade and DLF Emporio | Vasant Kunj, Delhi | About 0.8 msf combined; potential for ~0.5 msf more |
| DLF Avenue | Saket, Delhi | Potential for ~0.2 msf more GLA |
| DLF Cyber Hub | Cyber City, Gurugram | Dining and entertainment district |
| DLF Midtown Plaza | Moti Nagar, Delhi | Opened FY27; reported fully operational and highly leased |
| DLF Summit Plaza | DLF Phase 5, Gurugram | Soft-launched; reported ~90% leased |
| DLF Promenade, Goa | Goa | Received occupancy certificate; opening during FY27 |
| New Gurgaon Plaza | Sector 91, Gurugram | Neighbourhood plaza (announced) |

- The three new FY27 retail destinations (Midtown Plaza, Summit Plaza, Goa) total about 1.5 msf GLA.
- Retail contributes roughly 18% of DLF's rental income (2022 figure).

**Q: Can I lease a retail unit?** Yes; route to DLF Retail leasing with brand, category, size, and city.
