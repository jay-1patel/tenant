# Booking Process

1. **Initial Consultation** – Explore typologies and pricing.
2. **Site Visit** – Schedule a visit to model apartments.
3. **Expression of Interest** – Submit KYC and application.
4. **Booking Token** – ₹5–10 Lakhs (independent), ₹10–25 Lakhs (luxury), ₹50 Lakhs–₹1 Crore (super‑luxury).
5. **Milestone Payments** – As per construction milestones.
6. **Possession** – Final settlement and handover.

# Payment Plans
- **CLP** – Construction linked.
- **PLP** – 20‑30% upfront.
- **DPP** – Upfront payment with developer discount.
