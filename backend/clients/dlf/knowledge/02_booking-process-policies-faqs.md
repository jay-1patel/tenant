# Booking Process & Frequently Asked Questions

### How to book a site visit?
1. Choose the property you are interested in from the **Explore Offerings** menu.
2. Click **Book Site Visit** and provide your preferred date, time, and contact details.
3. Our sales team will confirm the appointment within 24 hours.

### What are the typical lease terms?
- Minimum lease period: **3 years**
- Security deposit: **10 % of annual rent**
- Rent escalation: **5 % per annum** (subject to negotiation)

### Is green energy available?
Yes. Several parks (e.g., DLF Cybercity Chennai & DLF Downtown Taramani) offer **100 % green energy** options at no additional cost.

### What documentation is required?
- Company PAN and GST registration
- Board resolution authorising lease
- Financial statements for the last 2 years

### Who to contact for pricing?
Select **Pricing & Leasing** from the main menu or type *price* followed by the property name. Our bot will route you to a sales executive for a customized quotation.
