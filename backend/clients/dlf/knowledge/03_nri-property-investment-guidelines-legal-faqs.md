# NRI & PIO Investment

- NRIs and PIOs can purchase residential/commercial property without RBI approval.
- No restriction on number of properties.
- No foreign national can acquire agricultural land by purchase.
- Gifts and inheritance rules are governed by FEMA.
- Transfer of property to NRIs/PIOs requires RBI approval if specific permission was needed.

# FAQs
- **Q:** Can NRIs buy property? **A:** Yes.
- **Q:** Are there any restrictions on number of properties? **A:** No.
- **Q:** Can a foreign national acquire property? **A:** Only residential lease up to 5 years.
