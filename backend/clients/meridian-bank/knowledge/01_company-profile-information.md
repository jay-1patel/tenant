# Meridian Bank\n\n# About Meridian Bank (Fictional Demo)

## Overview [DEMO]
- **Name:** Meridian Bank Limited.
- **Type:** Private sector scheduled commercial bank, regulated by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI).
- **Founded:** 2004. **Headquarters:** Gurugram, Haryana.
- **Network:** 620 branches, 1,800 ATMs, across 14 states.
- **Customers:** about 4.2 million retail and 60,000 business customers.
- **Tagline:** "Banking that keeps pace."

## Business lines
1. **Retail banking:** savings and salary accounts, deposits, cards, loans.
2. **Home finance:** home loans, balance transfer, loan against property. Tie-ups with major developers, including approved-project lists for leading builders. [DEMO]
3. **NRI banking:** NRE, NRO, FCNR(B) accounts and remittance services.
4. **Business banking:** current accounts, overdraft, working-capital loans, payment solutions.
5. **Digital banking:** Meridian Mobile app, net banking, UPI, WhatsApp banking.

## Regulation and safety (general, true for scheduled banks in India)
- Deposits are insured by DICGC up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank (principal + interest combined).
- The bank follows RBI KYC, anti-money laundering, and customer protection guidelines.

## Opening an account
- **Documents:** PAN (or Form 60), Aadhaar or other officially valid document for address, passport photo.
- **Ways to open:** branch visit, or video KYC through the app (resident Indians, 18+).
- Minors: a minor's account is opened with a parent/guardian. Joint accounts available (either or survivor, former or survivor, etc.).
