## Engagement Models

### Fixed-scope project
- Requirement freeze, milestone-based delivery
- Payment: 30% advance, remainder across milestones

### Dedicated team
- Monthly billing per team, minimum 3 months
- You direct priorities; we staff and manage the team

### Time & material
- Hourly billing, best for evolving scopes
- Fortnightly effort reports

## Process
1. Discovery call to understand requirements
2. Written proposal with scope, timeline, and commercial terms
3. Kick-off, weekly progress demos
4. UAT, deployment, and handover
5. Optional maintenance contract after go-live
