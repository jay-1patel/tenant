# L&T Finance — Customer Grievance Redressal & Escalation Matrix

L&T Finance is committed to delivering a transparent, fair, and prompt complaint resolution mechanism in line with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Fair Practices Code.

If a customer is dissatisfied with any service, statement, recovery behavior, or digital transaction, they can follow the structured 4-tier escalation hierarchy below:

---

## 🏛️ Official 4-Level Escalation Matrix

### Level 1: Frontline Customer Support
Customers should first register their concern with the primary customer care team:
- **Toll-Free Phone Helpline:** `1800 268 0000` (Mon–Sun, 09:00 AM – 06:00 PM, except National Holidays)
- **Email:** `customercare@ltfs.com`
- **Online Complaint Portal:** [https://www.ltfs.com/grievance-redressal](https://www.ltfs.com/grievance-redressal)
- **Branch Walk-In:** Submit a written complaint to the Branch Service Manager at any nearest L&T Finance branch.
- **Turnaround Time (TAT):** Within **7 working days** from ticket generation date.

---

### Level 2: Grievance Redressal Officer (GRO)
If the customer does not receive a satisfactory resolution within 7 working days from Level 1, or the ticket remains unresolved, they may escalate to the appointed Grievance Redressal Officer:
- **Designation:** Grievance Redressal Officer, L&T Finance Limited
- **Email:** `gro@ltfs.com`
- **Postal Address:**
  Grievance Redressal Officer,
  L&T Finance Limited,
  Brindavan, Plot No. 177, C.S.T Road, Kalina,
  Santacruz (East), Mumbai - 400 098, Maharashtra.
- **Turnaround Time (TAT):** Within **7 working days** of receipt of escalation.

---

### Level 3: Principal Nodal Officer (PNO)
If the resolution provided by the GRO does not meet expectations within 7 working days, the matter can be escalated to the Principal Nodal Officer:
- **Designation:** Principal Nodal Officer, L&T Finance Limited
- **Email:** `nodalofficer@ltfs.com`
- **Turnaround Time (TAT):** Within **7 working days**.

---

### Level 4: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Ombudsman
If the grievance is not redressed within **30 days** of initial filing, or if the customer is not satisfied with the resolution provided by the Company:
- The customer may appeal to the **RBI Ombudsman** under the Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (RB-IOS).
- **Online Complaint Portal:** [https://cms.rbi.org.in](https://cms.rbi.org.in)
- **Toll-Free Helpline:** 14448
- **Postal Address:**
  Centralised Receipt and Processing Centre (CRPC),
  Reserve Bank of India, 4th Floor, Sector 17,
  Chandigarh - 160 017.

---

## 🛡️ Debt Collection & Customer Protection Standards

- **Fair Recovery Practices:** All recovery agents and collection staff are bound by RBI guidelines. Representatives will contact borrowers only between **08:00 AM and 07:00 PM**.
- **No Harassment:** Intimidation, abusive language, inappropriate calls, or contact at unseemly hours is strictly prohibited and subject to severe disciplinary action.
- **Confidentiality:** Customer loan data is protected under banking confidentiality laws; OTPs and MPINs are never requested by L&T Finance staff.
