### Q5: How do I obtain my No Objection Certificate (NOC) / No Dues Certificate after closing my loan?
**A:** Once all outstanding principal, interest, and charges are paid in full:
- Your digital No Dues Certificate (NDC) / NOC becomes available for download on the **PLANET App** or via WhatsApp within **7 to 14 working days** after payment realization.
- For two-wheeler and tractor loans, the physical Form 35 (for RTO hypothecation removal) and NOC are dispatched via registered courier to your communication address on record.

### Q6: Can I prepay or foreclose my loan before the tenure ends?
**A:** Yes, you can prepay or foreclose your loan at any point:
- **Floating-Rate Loans to Individuals:** In accordance with RBI guidelines, there are **ZERO (0%) foreclosure / prepayment charges** on floating rate term loans availed by individual borrowers for non-business purposes.
- **Fixed-Rate Loans / Business Entities:** A nominal foreclosure fee of 2% to 5% + GST may apply on the outstanding principal as per your signed sanction letter.
To initiate closure, generate a Foreclosure Statement via the PLANET App or contact 1800-268-0000.

### Q8: What happens if my EMI auto-debit bounces?
**A:** If an auto-debit mandate bounces due to insufficient funds:
1. A NACH return / bounce fee of **₹450 to ₹600 + GST** is charged.
2. Overdue penal charges will accrue on the unpaid installment until paid.
3. The delay may be reported to credit rating bureaus (CIBIL/Experian), which can lower your credit score.
To prevent further charges, clear the dues immediately via [ltfs.com/quickpay](https://www.ltfs.com/quickpay).

### Q14: What is the Grievance Escalation Process if my issue is not resolved?
**A:** L&T Finance follows a transparent 4-level grievance resolution matrix:
- **Level 1:** Call Customer Care at `1800 268 0000` or email `customercare@ltfs.com`. TAT: 7 working days.
- **Level 2:** Escalate to the Grievance Redressal Officer at `gro@ltfs.com`. TAT: 7 working days.
- **Level 3:** Escalate to the Principal Nodal Officer at `nodalofficer@ltfs.com`. TAT: 7 working days.
- **Level 4:** If unresolved after 30 days, file an appeal with the **RBI Ombudsman** at [https://cms.rbi.org.in](https://cms.rbi.org.in) or call 14448.
