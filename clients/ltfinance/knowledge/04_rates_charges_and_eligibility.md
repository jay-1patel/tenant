## 2. Standard Fees & Schedule of Charges

### 1. Loan Processing Fee
- Typically ranges from **1% to 3%** of the sanctioned loan amount plus applicable GST.
- Deducted directly from loan disbursal proceeds or collected upfront during sanction.

### 2. EMI Bounce / NACH Return Charges
- If an auto-debit bounces due to insufficient balance or technical reasons, a bounce fee of **₹450 to ₹600 + GST** is levied per bounced presentation.
- Penal interest / late payment fees apply on the overdue amount for the delayed period as outlined in the loan agreement.

### 3. Prepayment & Foreclosure Charges
- **Floating Rate Term Loans (Individual Borrowers for non-business purposes):**
  - **NIL / 0% Foreclosure Fee** in strict compliance with RBI circulars.
- **Fixed Rate Loans / Non-Individual (Entity/Business) Borrowers:**
  - Foreclosure charges of **2% to 5% + GST** may apply on the outstanding principal depending on product terms and lock-in period.

### 4. Statement & Document Charges
- Digital statements downloaded via PLANET App / WhatsApp: **FREE OF COST (₹0)**.
- Physical duplicate copy requests at branch counters: Nominal charge (₹100–₹250 + GST).

## 3. General Eligibility Criteria

### Salaried Individuals:
- **Age:** 21 to 58 years (or retirement age).
- **Minimum Monthly Income:** ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 depending on city tier and loan type.
- **Employment Stability:** Minimum 1 year total work experience, with at least 6 months at current employer.
- **Credit Score:** Healthy CIBIL score of 700+ preferred.

### Self-Employed / Business Owners:
- **Age:** 21 to 65 years.
- **Business Vintage:** Minimum 2 to 3 years of continuous operational vintage.
- **Financial Proofs:** Past 2 years audited ITR with computation of income, GST returns, and 6–12 months active bank statements.

---

## 4. Documentation Checklist (KYC & Financials)

1. **Identity Proof:** PAN Card (Mandatory), Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID.
2. **Address Proof:** Aadhaar Card, Electricity Bill, Rental Agreement, or Passport.
3. **Income Proof:**
   - *Salaried:* Last 3 months salary slips, Form 16, last 6 months salary bank account statement.
   - *Self-Employed:* Last 2 years ITR, GST returns, last 6–12 months current/savings bank statements.
4. **Collateral Documents (for secured loans):**
   - *Home Loan / LAP:* Original title deeds, chain documents, approved sanction plan, tax receipts.
   - *Vehicle Loan:* Quotation / proforma invoice from authorized automobile dealer.
   - *Gold Loan:* Physical gold jewellery presented for on-the-spot karat evaluation.
