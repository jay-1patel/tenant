# L&T Finance — Comprehensive Loan Products Suite

L&T Finance offers a diverse portfolio of retail, rural, and MSME lending products designed for salaried, self-employed, and business customers across India.

---

## 1. Two-Wheeler Loans (Bike & Scooter Finance)

### Overview
Financing solutions for standard commuter motorcycles, scooters, electric two-wheelers (EVs), and premium sports/superbikes.

### Key Highlights:
- **Loan Amount:** Up to ₹20 Lakhs.
- **Funding:** Up to 100% on-road financing available on select models and profiles.
- **Approval Speed:** Instant in-principle digital approval in under 2 minutes at dealerships or online.
- **Repayment Tenure:** Flexible tenures ranging from 12 to 48 months (up to 60 months on premium bikes).
- **Special Features:** Special interest rate concessions for female co-borrowers, zero documentation paperless journeys with Aadhaar e-KYC.

## 2. Personal Loans / Consumer Loans

### Overview
Unsecured multi-purpose credit to meet planned life goals or unplanned emergencies without pledging any security or collateral.

### Key Highlights:
- **Loan Amount:** ₹50,000 up to ₹15 Lakhs (pre-approved offers up to ₹30 Lakhs).
- **Process:** 100% digital, paperless application through PLANET app or website.
- **Tenure:** 12 to 60 months.
- **Purposes Covered:**
  - Medical emergencies and hospital bills
  - Weddings and family celebrations
  - Home improvement, painting, and interior renovation
  - Travel and holiday expenses
  - Higher education and vocational certifications
  - High-cost debt consolidation
- **Disbursal:** Direct credited into verified bank account within 24 hours of digital verification.

## 3. Housing Finance (Home Loans & Loan Against Property)

### A. Home Loans
- **Target Segments:** Salaried professionals and self-employed individuals buying ready-to-move properties, under-construction apartments, or constructing on self-owned plots.
- **Tenure:** Long-term tenure up to 30 years to ensure pocket-friendly EMIs.
- **Facilities:** Balance Transfer (switch high-interest loans from other lenders) with Top-Up loan facility for extra liquidity.
- **Subsidies & Tax Benefits:** Eligible for deductions under Section 80C (principal) and Section 24(b) (interest) of the Income Tax Act.

### B. Loan Against Property (LAP)
- **Collateral:** Commercial or residential self-occupied property.
- **Loan Amount:** High-value funding up to ₹5 Crores for business expansion or long-term liquidity.
- **Tenure:** Up to 15 to 20 years.

## 4. Gold Loans (Instant Loan Against Gold Jewellery)

### Overview
Fast liquidity secured against 18-karat to 22-karat household gold ornaments with maximum loan-to-value (LTV) per RBI guidelines.

### Key Highlights:
- **Loan Amount:** Starting from ₹5,000 with no upper ceiling.
- **Interest Rates:** Highly competitive rates starting from **11.75%* p.a.**
- **Schemes Available:** *Shreyas, Sahaj, Sudarshan, Swarnim, Sunehra, Sparsh, Sulabh, Sampann, Shresth, and Shikhar*.
- **Tenure:** Up to 12 months with flexible repayment options (bullet repayment, monthly interest only, regular EMI).
- **Vault Security:** Gold is stored in bank-grade strongroom vaults under 24x7 CCTV electronic surveillance and full transit insurance.

## 5. SME & Business Loans

### Overview
Customized financing to power growth for micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs).

### Key Highlights:
- **Collateral-Free Lending:** Unsecured business loans up to ₹50 Lakhs based on banking turnover and GST returns.
- **End-Uses:** Working capital cycles, inventory procurement, equipment/machinery purchase, office expansion.
- **Tenure:** 12 to 36 months with structured repayment schedules.
- **Eligibility:** Businesses operational for at least 2–3 years with healthy GST/ITR compliance.

## 7. Rural Business Finance (Vikaas Loans / Micro Loans)

### Overview
Financial inclusion and microfinance initiatives aimed at women micro-entrepreneurs in rural and semi-urban India.

### Key Highlights:
- **Group Lending Model:** Joint Liability Group (JLG) loans fostering mutual support and financial discipline.
- **End-Uses:** Dairy farming, tailoring, kirana retail stores, handicrafts, and livestock rearing.
- **Doorstep Service:** Dedicated loan officers visiting rural village centres for transparent loan origination and collection.
