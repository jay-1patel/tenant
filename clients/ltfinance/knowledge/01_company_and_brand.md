## 4. Digital Platforms: PLANET by L&T Finance
- **App Name:** PLANET by L&T Finance (available on Android Google Play Store and Apple iOS App Store).
- **Core Functionality:**
  - View all active and closed loan accounts.
  - Download Statement of Account (SOA), Repayment Schedule, and Interest Certificates.
  - Track upcoming EMI dates and pay outstanding EMIs instantly via UPI/NetBanking.
  - Apply for pre-approved top-up loans and new loan products.
  - Request No Objection Certificates (NOC) and track physical courier dispatch.
