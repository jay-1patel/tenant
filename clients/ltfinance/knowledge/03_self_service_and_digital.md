# L&T Finance — Digital Self-Service & Loan Management

Customers can access instant, paperless account services 24x7 through the **WhatsApp Assistant (+91 7378333451)**, the **PLANET by L&T Finance App**, or the web portal **[ltfs.com/customer-service](https://www.ltfs.com/customer-service)**.

---

## 1. Document Downloads & Instant Statements

### 1. Statement of Account (SOA)
- **What it is:** Complete chronological record of all debits, credits, interest charged, fees, and balance principal on your loan account.
- **How to download:**
  - *On WhatsApp:* Type "SOA" or "Statement of Account", authenticate via OTP sent to registered mobile number, and receive PDF directly.
  - *On PLANET App:* Go to *My Loans* → Select Loan Account Number (LAN) → Tap *Download Statement*.
  - *By Email:* Sent to your registered email ID within minutes.

### 2. Repayment Schedule (Amortization Chart)
- **What it is:** Detailed month-by-month breakup showing upcoming EMI due dates, principal component, interest component, and outstanding balance after each installment.
- **How to download:** Available instantly in PLANET App under *Loan Details* → *Amortization Schedule*.

### 3. Interest Certificate (Tax Exemption Certificate)
- **What it is:** Certified breakdown of total interest and principal repaid during the financial year, used for Income Tax filing under Section 24(b) and Section 80C.
- **Availability:** Both *Provisional Interest Certificate* (for ongoing fiscal year) and *Final Interest Certificate* (for concluded fiscal year) can be downloaded via PLANET App or WhatsApp.

### 4. Foreclosure Statement / Settlement Quote
- **What it is:** Official statement specifying total outstanding dues payable to prepay and permanently close the loan account as on a given date.
- **Validity:** Typically valid for 15 to 30 days from generation date.
- **Request Process:** Request via PLANET App or raise a ticket on customer portal.

### 5. No Objection Certificate (NOC) / No Dues Certificate (NDC)
- **What it is:** Legal document issued upon complete loan repayment confirming that all outstanding dues have been cleared and hypothecation/lien is released.
- **Turnaround Time:**
  - Soft copy available for download via PLANET App / WhatsApp within **7 to 14 working days** after clearance of the final EMI / closure payment.
  - For vehicle loans (Two-Wheeler, Tractor), Form 35 is dispatched to the registered postal address for RTO hypothecation removal.

## 2. Online EMI Payment & Quick Pay

Customers who missed an auto-debit or wish to pay advance installments can pay instantly online:

### Payment Methods Supported:
1. **Quick Pay Portal:** Visit [https://www.ltfs.com/quickpay](https://www.ltfs.com/quickpay)
2. **UPI:** Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM UPI (using Loan Account Number as reference).
3. **Net Banking / Debit Cards:** Supported across all major Indian scheduled banks.
4. **PLANET App:** One-click instant clearing via saved UPI IDs or payment gateways.

### Important Payment Advice:
- Always ensure payments are made through official L&T Finance channels only.
- Never transfer EMI amounts into personal accounts or personal UPI IDs of collection agents.
- Digital receipts are sent via SMS / WhatsApp instantly upon successful payment.

## 3. Bank Account & Auto-Debit Mandate (NACH / e-Mandate)

- **Standard Debit Mode:** Auto-debit via National Automated Clearing House (NACH) / e-Mandate linked to the borrower’s primary bank account.
- **EMI Due Date:** Typically between the 1st and 10th of every month depending on the loan contract.
- **Updating Bank Account:**
  - If you changed your bank account, submit an e-Mandate update request via the PLANET App or visit the nearest L&T Finance branch with a cancelled cheque and bank statement at least **15 days prior** to the next EMI date.
