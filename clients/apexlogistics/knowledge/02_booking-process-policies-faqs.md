# Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What documents are required for international customs clearance?
**A:** Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading or Airway Bill, and Certificate of Origin.

### Q: Can I track my cargo in real‑time?
**A:** Yes. Every shipment receives a 12‑digit tracking AWB number accessible via WhatsApp and our online portal.

### Q: What is the claim and refund policy for transit damage?
**A:** Insured consignments can file claims within 7 days of delivery. Submit inspection photographs and claim form to initiate the refund process.

### Q: How do I request a price quote?
**A:** Use the *Request Quote* menu option or contact us at care@apexlogistics.com with shipment details (origin, destination, weight, dimensions).

### Q: What are your operating hours?
**A:** Monday to Saturday, 09:00 AM – 07:00 PM (Asia/Kolkata).
