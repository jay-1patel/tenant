## 2. Spiritual, Temple & Jyotirlinga Circuits
1. **Jyotirling Special (5 Days / 4 Nights):**
   - Dedicated pilgrimage to the two sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva in Gujarat: **Somnath Temple** and **Nageshwar Jyotirlinga**, plus Dwarkadhish temple and Gomti Ghat.
2. **Panch Dwarka (10 Days / 9 Nights):**
   - Complete pilgrimage covering all five Dwarka shrines: Gomti Dwarka (Dwarkadhish), Bet Dwarka (Shankhodhar), Rukmini Dwarka, Dakor (Ranchhodraiji), and Shrinathji Nathdwara / Kankroli connection.
3. **Krishna's Gujarat & Gujarat Krishna Trail (4 Days & 12 Days):**
   - Retracing the footsteps of Lord Krishna through Dwarka, Bet Dwarka, Gopi Talav, Sudama Puri Porbandar, Bhalka Tirth, and Triveni Sangam Somnath.
4. **Somnath Pilgrimage (4 Days / 3 Nights):**
   - Deep spiritual stay in Somnath, Triveni Sangam, Geeta Mandir, Bhalka Tirth, and excursion to Gir or Diu.
5. **Jain Temple Tour (8 Days / 7 Nights):**
   - Holy Jain pilgrimage covering Palitana (Shatrunjaya Hills temples), Girnar Jain temples, Taranga Hill, Shankheshwar Parshwanath, and Mahudi.
6. **Pavagadh Pilgrimage (3 Days / 2 Nights):**
   - Maa Mahakalika Temple at Pavagadh Hill, ropeway darshan, and UNESCO World Heritage Champaner-Pavagadh Archaeological Park.
7. **Nishkalank Mahadev (3 Days / 2 Nights):**
   - Miraculous Pandava sea-temple at Koliyak near Bhavnagar, submerged during high tide and accessible on foot during low tide, paired with Bhavnagar city heritage.
8. **Spiritual Gujarat (5 Days / 4 Nights):**
   - Ahmedabad, Ambaji Temple, Sun Temple Modhera, Rani ki Vav, and Shamlaji.
9. **Buddha Circuit (9 Days / 8 Nights):**
   - Exploring ancient Buddhist rock-cut caves: Uparkot Caves (Junagadh), Khambhalida Caves, Talaja Caves, and Sana Caves.

## 3. Wildlife, Eco & Nature Tours
1. **Lions Calling (3 Days / 2 Nights):**
   - *Destination:* Sasan Gir National Park & Devalia Safari Park. The only natural habitat of the Asiatic Lion (*Panthera leo leo*) on earth. Includes jungle safari permit assistance, nature walks, and resort stay.
2. **Velavadar Blackbuck National Park (3 Days / 2 Nights):**
   - Famous grassland ecosystem near Bhavnagar, home to massive herds of endangered Blackbucks, Indian Wolves, Striped Hyenas, and Lesser Floricans.
3. **Gujarat Birdscape (10 Days / 9 Nights):**
   - Birdwatching paradise covering Nal Sarovar Bird Sanctuary, Little Rann of Kutch (Wild Ass Sanctuary & flamingos), Khijadiya Bird Sanctuary Jamnagar, and Porbandar wetlands.
4. **Gujarat Wildcoast (5 Days / 4 Nights):**
   - Marine National Park at Narara (Jamnagar) for walking marine reef safaris, coastal mangroves, and dolphin sightings.
5. **Eco Campsite Tour (6 Days / 5 Nights):**
   - Community-based eco-campsites including Polo Forest, Mahal Campsite in Dang, Padamdungari, and Vansda National Park.

## 4. Kutch & Desert Experiences
1. **Rann Utsav Special (3 Days / 2 Nights):**
   - White Salt Desert of Kutch festival at Dhordo Tent City, camel safaris, cultural performances, and sunset/sunrise on the salt flats.
2. **Best of Kutch (8 Days / 7 Nights) & Classic Kutch (5 Days):**
   - Bhuj (Aina Mahal, Prag Mahal, Kutch Museum), Dhordo White Rann, Kala Dungar (Black Hill - highest point of Kutch), handicraft artisan villages (Hodka, Nirona Rogan art, Bhujodi weaving), and Mandvi beach.
3. **Kutch Heritage Tour (8 Days / 7 Nights) & Kutch Special (6 Days):**
   - Includes historical Harappan UNESCO site at Dholavira, fossil park, and Chhari Dhand wetland reserve.

## 5. Heritage, Palaces & Monuments
1. **Statue of Unity (3 Days / 2 Nights):**
   - World's tallest statue (182 metres) honoring Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel at Kevadia. Includes Viewing Gallery ticket assistance, Valley of Flowers, Sardar Sarovar Dam, Glow Garden, Jungle Safari, and evening Light & Sound Projection Show.
2. **Gujarat Heritage Site (7 Days / 6 Nights) & UNESCO Sites (9 Days):**
   - World Heritage City Ahmedabad (heritage walks, pols, Sidi Saiyyed Mosque), Rani Ki Vav (11th-century stepwell at Patan), Champaner-Pavagadh, and Dholavira.
3. **Classic Vadodara (6 Days / 5 Nights):**
   - Vadodara city, Laxmi Vilas Palace (4 times the size of Buckingham Palace), Sayaji Baug, Baroda Museum & Picture Gallery, and nearby Statue of Unity.
4. **Stepwell Tour (8 Days / 7 Nights):**
   - Showcasing ancient subterranean water architecture: Rani ki Vav (Patan), Adalaj Ni Vav (Gandhinagar), Dada Harir Stepwell (Ahmedabad), and Navghan Kuvo (Junagadh).
5. **Gujarat Textile Tour (9 Days / 8 Nights):**
   - Exploring world-renowned Gujarati textiles: Patola double-ikat silk of Patan, Ajrakh block printing, Bandhani tie-dye of Jamnagar, and Kutch embroidery.

2. **Diu Island Gateway (3 Days / 2 Nights):**
