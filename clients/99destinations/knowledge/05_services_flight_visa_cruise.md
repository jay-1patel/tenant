## 2. Worldwide Visa Assistance
99 Destinations handles visa processing for virtually any country requiring tourist, business, or transit visas for Indian passport holders.

### Services Provided:
1. **Up-to-Date Documentation Guidance:**
   - Country-specific checklists, latest visa fee tables, and current embassy appointment slots.
2. **Form Filling & Appointment Scheduling:**
   - Professional completion of visa applications (US DS-160, UK visa portal, Schengen VFS/TLS forms, Canadian portals, e-Visas).
3. **Application Verification & Scrutiny:**
   - Complete pre-submission audit of passport validity, photographs, 6-month bank statements, ITR returns, employment/business proof, leave letters, and travel vouchers to prevent common rejection triggers.
4. **Door-to-Door Document Delivery:**
   - Secure courier dispatch from consulate/embassy locations directly to client doorsteps.
5. **Popular Destinations Handled:**
   - **Schengen Europe** (France, Switzerland, Austria, Italy, Germany, Netherlands, Spain)
   - **United Arab Emirates (Dubai 14/30/60 days e-Visa)**
   - **United States (B1/B2 tourist/business visa)**
   - **United Kingdom (Standard visitor visa)**
   - **Southeast Asia** (Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia/Bali)
   - **Australia & New Zealand**
   - **Eurasia & Middle East** (Turkey, Georgia, Azerbaijan, Armenia, Oman, Saudi Arabia)

*Important Disclaimer:* While 99 Destinations provides thorough documentation and scrutiny, the grant or refusal of a visa is the sole legal prerogative of the respective embassy/consulate. 99 Destinations never guarantees visa approval.
