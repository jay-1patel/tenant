# 99 Destinations Holidays — Indian Holidays & International Tours

Beyond its signature Gujarat Tourism division, 99 Destinations Holidays Pvt. Ltd. provides complete holiday planning, transportation, hotel bookings, and guided tours across India and around the globe.

---

## 1. Indian Holidays (Domestic Destinations)

### 1. Kashmir & Leh Ladakh
- **Classic Kashmir (8 Days / 7 Nights):**
  - *Route:* Srinagar (Dal Lake Shikara ride, Mughal Gardens, Houseboat stay) → Gulmarg (Gondola cable car ride to Apharwat Peak) → Pahalgam (Betaab Valley, Aru Valley) → Sonmarg (Thajiwas Glacier).
- **Leh Ladakh — Roof Top Drive (7 Days / 6 Nights):**
  - *Route:* Leh → Sham Valley (Magnetic Hill, Sangam of Indus & Zanskar rivers, Pathar Sahib Gurudwara) → Khardung La Pass (world’s highest motorable pass) → Nubra Valley (Hunder sand dunes & double-humped Bactrian camels, Diskit Monastery) → Pangong Tso Lake (high-altitude color-changing lake) → Leh.

### 2. Rajasthan Royal Heritage
- **Classic Rajasthan (10 Days / 9 Nights):**
  - *Route:* Jaipur (Amer Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace) → Jodhpur (Mehrangarh Fort, Umaid Bhawan Palace) → Jaisalmer (Golden Fort, Sam Sand Dunes desert camping & camel safari) → Udaipur (City Palace, Lake Pichola boat cruise, Jag Mandir).

### 3. Kerala Backwaters & Hill Stations
- **God's Own Country (6 Days / 5 Nights to 8 Days / 7 Nights):**
  - *Route:* Cochin → Munnar (tea plantations, Mattupetty Dam, Eravikulam National Park) → Thekkady (Periyar wildlife boat safari, spice plantations) → Alleppey / Kumarakom (deluxe traditional houseboat cruise & overnight backwater stay) → Kovalam / Trivandrum beaches.

### 4. Andaman & Nicobar Islands
- **Andaman Tropical Getaway (5 Days to 7 Days):**
  - *Route:* Port Blair (Cellular Jail Light & Sound show, Corbyn's Cove beach) → Private ferry to Havelock Island (Radhanagar Beach — Asia's best beach, Elephant Beach snorkeling & scuba diving) → Neil Island (natural rock bridge, Bharatpur beach).

### 5. Cordelia Cruises (India's Premier Luxury Cruise)
- **Cordelia Cruise Experience (3 Days to 6 Days):**
  - *Routes:* Mumbai → Goa → Lakshadweep (Kadmat / Agatti islands) → Mumbai; Mumbai to Diu; Kochi to Lakshadweep.
  - *Ship Amenities:* Luxury ocean-view staterooms and suites, multi-cuisine dining, casinos, Broadway-style theatrical shows, night clubs, swimming pools, rock climbing wall, and spa.

### 6. North East, Sikkim & Orissa
- **Classic Tripura (5 Days) & Best of Sikkim (7 Days):**
  - Agartala (Ujjayanta Palace, Neermahal water palace, Unakoti rock carvings) and Gangtok (Tsomgo Lake, Baba Mandir, Nathula Pass, Pelling).
- **Classic Orissa & Pride of Orissa (5 Days & 7 Days):**
  - Bhubaneswar (temple city) → Puri (Lord Jagannath Temple, Golden Beach) → Konark (Sun Temple UNESCO site) → Chilika Lake (Irrawaddy dolphin sanctuary).

## 2. International Tours

### 1. Dazzling Dubai (UAE) — 7 Days / 6 Nights
- *Highlights:*
  - Dubai City Tour, Dubai Frame, Burj Khalifa 124th floor observation deck with Dubai Mall fountain show.
  - Desert Safari with 4x4 dune bashing, BBQ dinner, Tanoura dance, and belly dancing.
  - Marina Dhow Cruise with international buffet dinner.
  - Miracle Garden, Global Village, Future Museum, and day excursion to Abu Dhabi (Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque & Ferrari World / Louvre).

### 2. Switzerland Special — 8 Days / 7 Nights
- *Highlights:*
  - Zurich & Lucerne (Chapel Bridge, Lion Monument, Lake Lucerne boat cruise).
  - Mount Titlis with Rotair revolving cable car and ice cliff walk.
  - Interlaken & Jungfraujoch — Top of Europe (cogwheel train to the highest railway station in Europe).
  - Scenic Swiss panoramic train rides (GoldenPass or Glacier Express sections) and Rhine Falls at Schaffhausen.

### 3. Jewels of Austria & Central Europe — 13 Days / 12 Nights
- *Highlights:*
  - Vienna (Schönbrunn Palace, St. Stephen’s Cathedral, Ringstrasse).
  - Salzburg (birthplace of Mozart, Sound of Music tour, Mirabell Gardens).
  - Innsbruck & Swarovski Crystal Worlds in Wattens.
  - Grand combination circuits with Paris, Switzerland, and Italy (Rome, Florence, Venice).

### 4. Turkey Golden Triangle — 8 Days / 7 Nights
- *Highlights:*
  - Istanbul (Hagia Sophia, Blue Mosque, Topkapi Palace, Grand Bazaar, Bosphorus Cruise).
  - Cappadocia (fairy chimneys, underground cities, hot air balloon ride at sunrise).
  - Pamukkale (cotton castle thermal travertines & ancient Hierapolis).

### 5. Stunning Georgia — 7 Days / 6 Nights
- *Highlights:*
  - Tbilisi (Old Town, Narikala Fortress, Sulphur Baths, Bridge of Peace).
  - Kazbegi & Caucasian mountain valleys (Gergeti Trinity Church).
  - Kakheti wine region (ancient Georgian wine cellars) and Mtskheta UNESCO ancient capital.

### 6. Ultimate USA — 16 Days / 15 Nights
- *Highlights:*
  - East Coast: New York (Statue of Liberty, Times Square, Empire State), Washington DC (Capitol, Smithsonian museums, White House), Niagara Falls (Maid of the Mist boat tour).
  - West Coast: Las Vegas (world-famous Strip casinos, Grand Canyon helicopter/coach tour), Los Angeles (Hollywood Walk of Fame, Universal Studios), San Francisco (Golden Gate Bridge, Cable Car, Fisherman's Wharf).

- **GIT (Group Inclusive Tour):** Fixed departure dates with a dedicated Gujarati/Hindi/English speaking tour manager, group meals (including pure vegetarian and Jain meals on international tours).
- **MICE & Corporate Tours:** Incentive trips, team building retreats, and conferences with dedicated coordinators.
