# 99 Destinations Holidays — Special Attractions & Heritage Stays

## 1. Seema Darshan — Nada Bet (Indo-Pak Border Outpost)

### Overview & Significance
Seema Darshan at Nada Bet is a frontier military tourism program organized by Gujarat Tourism in Banaskantha district near Suigam. Set against vast white desert flats and seasonal wetlands, Nada Bet gives travelers a rare and patriotic insight into the vigil and valor of the Border Security Force (BSF) guarding India’s international border.

### Key Highlights & Attractions:
1. **Sunset Retreat Ceremony:**
   - The crowning attraction of Seema Darshan is the ceremonial Beating Retreat parade performed at sunset by smartly turned-out BSF jawans in full regalia, echoing national pride and precision.
2. **Weapons & Military Exhibits:**
   - Visitors can see operational and historic border defense equipment, artillery exhibits, armored carriers, and informative galleries displaying the evolution of border surveillance.
3. **Camel Show & BSF Showmanship:**
   - Spectacular camel parade and musical acrobatics presented by the BSF Camel Wing, celebrating the traditional desert guardians.
4. **Watch Tower & Zero Point Observation:**
   - Viewpoints overlooking the actual international border fence and frontier outposts.
5. **Interactive Experience Centers:**
   - Audio-visual museum exhibits showcasing war history, border patrol challenges, and heroic stories of Indian soldiers.

## 2. Rann Utsav — Kutch White Desert Festival

### The Magic of the White Rann
Rann Utsav is one of India's most celebrated cultural festivals, held annually from November to February in the White Salt Desert of Dhordo, Kutch. Under the winter moonlight, especially on full moon nights, the salt crystals illuminate like a sea of silver.

### Official Accommodations at The Tent City (Dhordo):
99 Destinations organizes official reservations across luxury tent categories:
1. **Darbari Suite:**
   - The pinnacle of imperial luxury. Sprawling royal living rooms, master bedrooms, antique-style furnishings, handcrafted Kutchi textiles, private dining space, and personalized hospitality.
2. **Rajwadi Suite:**
   - Regal heritage ambiance designed for luxury travelers, featuring spacious interiors, plush bedding, premium bath amenities, and authentic Gujarat craftsmanship.
3. **Super Premium Tents:**
   - Contemporary glamping with wooden flooring, climate-controlled air conditioning, plush lounge area, and seamless connectivity to central festival arenas.
4. **Premium Tents:**
   - Comfortable and elegantly appointed luxury tents featuring wooden accents, private bathrooms, cozy beddings, and verandahs.
5. **Deluxe AC Swiss Cottages:**
   - Traditional Swiss cottage layout with modern air conditioning, comfortable furnishings, and private ensuite facilities.

### Rann Utsav Activities & Experiences:
- **Cultural Evenings:** Nightly folk dance (Garba, Dandiya, Sufi music, Bhavai) by local Kutchi troupes around bonfires.
- **Desert Adventures:** Paramotoring, ATV quad biking, desert camel cart rides, and hot air ballooning over the white flats.
- **Handicraft Shopping:** Direct artisan haats showcasing Rogan art (Nirona), Ajrakh block printing, copper bells, leather goods, and mirror embroidery.
- **Sunset & Full Moon Walks:** Guided walks into the heart of the salt desert for spectacular sunsets and magical full moon vistas.
- **Kala Dungar & Gandhi nu Gam Excursions:** Tours to the Black Hill (highest point of Kutch) and artisan villages.

## 3. Heritage Stays in Gujarat

### Bhavnagar Heritage Palaces
Bhavnagar, the headquarters of 99 Destinations, was a progressive princely state ruled by the Gohil Rajput dynasty.
- **Nilambag Palace Hotel:**
  - A 19th-century royal palace designed by a German architect, blending European and Indian Rajput architecture. Features grand banquet halls, antique chandeliers, lush royal gardens, and royal dining.
- **Narayan Baug & Royal Residences:**
  - Preserved properties reflecting Saurashtra's regal past, traditional court architecture, and legendary royal hospitality.

### Ahmedabad Heritage Mansions & Havelis
Ahmedabad is India's first UNESCO World Heritage City (inscribed in 2017).
- **Historic Walled City Pol Havelis:**
  - Restored wooden mansions (*havelis*) nestled inside the ancient *pols* (gated micro-neighborhoods). Intricately carved wooden facades, central courtyards, rain-water harvesting tanks (*tanka*), and authentic Gujarati vegetarian feasts.
- **Palatial Boutique Stays:**
  - Luxury heritage retreats on the banks of the Sabarmati River and surrounding outskirts, offering tranquil royal courtyards, brass artifacts, and guided heritage walking tours.

## 4. Top Gujarat Cities & What to Visit

1. **Ahmedabad (UNESCO World Heritage City):**
   - Sabarmati Ashram (Mahatma Gandhi's home), Sidi Saiyyed Mosque (stone lattice tree of life), Adalaj Stepwell, Hutheesing Jain Temple, Akshardham Gandhinagar, and Sabarmati Riverfront.
2. **Bhavnagar:**
   - Takhteshwar Temple (panoramic hill shrine), Barton Library & Museum, Victoria Park, Gaurishankar Lake, and excursion to Nishkalank Mahadev (Koliyak) & Velavadar Blackbuck National Park.
3. **Dwarka (Char Dham Pilgrimage):**
   - Dwarkadhish Temple (Jagat Mandir), Bet Dwarka Island (ferry from Okha), Nageshwar Jyotirlinga, Gopi Talav, Rukmini Devi Temple, and Gomti Ghat evening aarti.
4. **Somnath:**
   - Somnath Mahadev Temple (first of the 12 Jyotirlingas), Seaside Promenade, Sound & Light Show, Triveni Sangam (confluence of Hiran, Kapila, and Saraswati rivers), and Bhalka Tirth.
5. **Kutch & Bhuj:**
   - White Rann at Dhordo, Bhuj Prag Mahal & Aina Mahal, Kala Dungar, Mandvi Beach & Vijay Vilas Palace, and Dholavira Harappan civilization site.
6. **Vadodara (Baroda):**
   - Laxmi Vilas Palace (grandest palace in India), Maharaja Fateh Singh Museum, Sayaji Baug, EME Temple, and gateway to Statue of Unity (Kevadia).
7. **Junagadh:**
   - Uparkot Fort, Buddhist rock-cut caves, ornate Mahabat Maqbara, Ashokan Rock Edicts, and Mount Girnar (sacred pilgrimage steps & ropeway).
