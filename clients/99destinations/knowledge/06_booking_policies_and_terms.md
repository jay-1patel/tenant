# 99 Destinations Holidays — Booking, Payment & Cancellation Policies

All bookings with 99 DESTINATIONS HOLIDAYS PVT. LTD. are governed by the official terms and conditions published below.

---

## 1. Booking & Payment Terms

### Payment Schedule:
1. **At the Time of Confirmation:**
   - **50% of the total package cost** is required as advance payment to secure hotel reservations, private vehicles, and tour allocations.
2. **20 Days Prior to Departure:**
   - The **balance 50% payment** must be cleared in full at least 20 days before the tour commencement date.
3. **Bookings Made Within 20 Days of Travel:**
   - **100% advance payment** is required at the time of booking confirmation.

### Scope of Activity:
- Initial quotes are indicative and subject to availability; services and room allotments are confirmed only upon receipt of the advance payment.
- Hotels quoted are in the base category unless specifically upgraded in the formal quote.

---

## 2. Cancellation Policy & Charges Schedule

If a tour participant cancels a booking, written intimation must be submitted to 99 Destinations during official office hours. Cancellation charges apply as follows:

| Notice Period Before Arrival Date | Cancellation Charge (% of Tour Cost) | Refund Applicable (% of Tour Cost) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **45 Days or more prior to arrival** | **10% of tour cost** | **90% refund** |
| **15 to 44 Days prior to arrival** | **25% of tour cost** | **75% refund** |
| **07 to 14 Days prior to arrival** | **50% of tour cost** | **50% refund** |
| **Less than 48 Hours prior or No-Show** | **100% of tour cost** | **No Refund (0%)** |

### Critical Cancellation Rules:
- **Written Intimation Mandatory:** All cancellations must be formally communicated in writing (via email or registered letter) by the person who signed the booking contract.
- **Office Working Days Only:** Cancellations are recorded on official working days (Monday to Saturday, 10:30 AM to 07:30 PM). **Any cancellation sent on a Sunday will be processed and calculated as received on the next working day (Monday).**
- **Third-Party Operators:** In the event of third-party operator services (e.g. specialized cruises, international hotel chains, jungle safaris), the third-party operator’s cancellation rules apply in addition to 99 Destinations rules.

---

## 3. Refund Timelines & Mode

- **Mode of Refund:** All refunds are paid directly by account payee cheque or electronic bank transfer in Indian Rupees (INR), regardless of the currency used at booking.
- **Processing Timeframe:**
  - Standard land package refund: **Approximately 45 days** from date of approved cancellation.
  - If airline, train, or cruise tickets have already been issued: **Approximately 60 days** to allow for carrier credit note processing.
- **No Refund for Unutilized Services:** No refund is admissible if a participant fails to join the group, arrives late, departs early, or does not consume included meals, hotel nights, sightseeing excursions, or transfers due to personal reasons, sickness, or flight delays.

---

## 4. Age Categories & Child Policy

1. **Infant:** Any child below **2 years of age** is classified as an Infant.
2. **Child (Under 12 Years):**
   - Children between **2 and 12 years** can book with an extra bed or at the special *'Child Without Bed'* rate.
   - *Important Condition:* Any child paying the 'Without Bed' rate will strictly not be provided a bed in hotel rooms.
3. **Adult (12 Years & Above):**
   - Any child reaching or exceeding **12 years of age** is legally and commercially classified as an **Adult** and charged full adult package rates.

---

## 5. Hotel Accommodation & Rooming

- Standard check-in is typically 12:00 PM / 02:00 PM and check-out is 10:00 AM / 11:00 AM depending on property rules.
- As hotel rooms have size limitations, a maximum of 3 adults are recommended per room.
- Triple rooms are twin/double-bedded rooms with a rollaway cot or floor mattress provided for the third person.
- In hill stations or coastal areas with mild weather, certain hotels do not feature air conditioning as standard.

---

## 6. Meals Policy

- Meals provided on tours follow pre-set fixed menus.
- While specific dietary requests (e.g. pure Jain food, vegan, or infant meals) will be accommodated where feasible, they cannot be guaranteed under all circumstance.
- No monetary refund or compensation is claimable for meals missed due to personal preference, flight delays, or late arrivals.

---

## 7. Coaches, Transportation & Baggage

- Vehicles operate on commercial passenger permits (*T-Permit*) subject to respective state laws.
- Daily seat rotation is enforced on group coach journeys to ensure fairness to all travelers.
- Standard economy class airline baggage allowance is 15 kg to 20 kg per person for domestic flights, and 1 to 2 pieces (23 kg each) for international sectors, per carrier rules.
- Baggage and personal belongings remain under the custody and responsibility of the traveler at all times. 99 Destinations is not liable for baggage lost or damaged by airlines, trains, cruise lines, or transit vehicles.

---

## 8. Visa & Travel Documents

- Travelers are solely responsible for ensuring passport validity (minimum 6 months from travel return date) and obtaining appropriate visas.
- In the event an embassy rejects or delays a visa application, 99 Destinations cannot be held liable. Standard cancellation fees apply; however, clients may transfer to alternative available dates by paying applicable supplier modification charges.
- 99 Destinations is not liable for transmission delays or errors committed by embassy consular officers.

---

## 9. Liability Limitations & Grievances

- 99 Destinations acts as a tour organizer connecting independent third-party service providers (hotels, airlines, coaches, cruise lines). The company is not liable for accidents, injuries, sickness, theft, or force majeure events (strikes, weather, roadblocks) beyond its control.
- Maximum liability of the company in any dispute is strictly limited to the total net amount received for the tour.
- **Complaint Window:** Any service grievance during the tour must be immediately notified in writing to the tour manager/coordinator. Formal claims must be submitted to 99 Destinations within **28 days** of tour completion; no claim notified after 28 days will be entertained.
- **Jurisdiction:** All contractual disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in **Bhavnagar, Gujarat, India**.
