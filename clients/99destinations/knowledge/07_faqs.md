### Q9: What is your cancellation and refund policy?
**A:** Cancellation fees are calculated based on when your written request is received:
- **45+ Days prior to arrival:** 10% fee (90% refund)
- **15 to 44 Days prior to arrival:** 25% fee (75% refund)
- **07 to 14 Days prior to arrival:** 50% fee (50% refund)
- **Less than 48 Hours prior or No-Show:** 100% fee (No refund)

*Important Rule:* Cancellations must be in writing during office working days (Mon–Sat). Requests sent on Sunday are processed on Monday.

### Q10: How long does it take to process a refund?
**A:** Approved refunds are processed directly by account payee cheque or electronic bank transfer in Indian Rupees (INR). Standard land package refunds take approximately **45 days**. If flight, train, or cruise tickets have already been issued, processing takes up to **60 days** to allow for carrier credit notes.

### Q11: What is Seema Darshan at Nada Bet?
**A:** Seema Darshan is an initiative by Gujarat Tourism located at Nada Bet near Suigam in Banaskantha along the Indo-Pak border. Visitors can witness the daily sunset Retreat Ceremony performed by Border Security Force (BSF) jawans, explore weapon galleries and military exhibits, watch camel shows, and visit the border watch tower.
