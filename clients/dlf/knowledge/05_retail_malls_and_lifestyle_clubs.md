# DLF Retail & Hospitality — Iconic Destinations & Lifestyle Clubs

DLF curates India's premier social, fashion, shopping, dining, and championship sports hubs.

## 1. Flagship Retail Destinations
### DLF Mall of India (Noida (Delhi NCR))
- **Location**: Sector 18
- **Mall Profile**: Destination Mega Mall (2 Million sq. ft.)
- **Highlights**: India's largest destination mall with 330+ international and national brands, 7 floors divided into 5 customized zones (Market Place, International Boulevard, The High Street, Family World, Leisure Zone).

### DLF Emporio (New Delhi)
- **Location**: Vasant Kunj
- **Mall Profile**: Ultra-Luxury Fashion Mall (320,000 sq. ft.)
- **Highlights**: India's undisputed palace of luxury shopping housing 65+ haute couture brands: Louis Vuitton, Dior, Gucci, Cartier, Giorgio Armani, Burberry, Tom Ford, Jimmy Choo.

### The Chanakya (New Delhi)
- **Location**: Chanakyapuri Diplomatic Enclave
- **Mall Profile**: Boutique Luxury & Dining Destination (189,000 sq. ft.)
- **Highlights**: Ultra-luxe destination featuring luxury global brands, PVR ECX 3-screen luxury cinema, and food hall / gourmet dining in the heart of Delhi's diplomatic zone.

### DLF Promenade (New Delhi)
- **Location**: Vasant Kunj
- **Mall Profile**: High Fashion & Lifestyle Mall (460,000 sq. ft.)
- **Highlights**: Trendy high-fashion shopping center with Zara, Sephora, Marks & Spencer, PVR ICON multiplex, and premium outdoor dining plaza.

### DLF Avenue (New Delhi)
- **Location**: Saket District Centre
- **Mall Profile**: Social Shopping & Dining Hub (520,000 sq. ft.)
- **Highlights**: Modernized millennial shopping haven featuring COMMONS (curated multi-cuisine dining district), Uniqlo flagship, high-street brands, and lively outdoor cultural events.

### DLF CyberHub (Gurugram)
- **Location**: DLF Cybercity Phase II
- **Mall Profile**: F&B, Social & Entertainment Hub (400,000 sq. ft.)
- **Highlights**: India's premier open-air social, dining and nightlife destination featuring 80+ cafes, fine dining restaurants, microbreweries, rooftop lounges, and open amphitheater.


## 2. Championship Golf & Residential Lifestyle Clubs
### DLF Golf & Country Club (Gurugram)
- **Location**: Golf Course Road, DLF Phase 5
- **Facility Type**: Championship Golf & Country Club
- **Experience & Inclusions**: India's most prestigious golf course featuring two 18-hole championship courses: the original Arnold Palmer Course and the legendary Gary Player Course with floodlit night golf, dramatic quarry features, and state-of-the-art golf academy.

### Club 5, Club 4, Club 3 & Club 2 (Gurugram)
- **Location**: DLF City Phases II, III, IV & V
- **Facility Type**: Exclusive Lifestyle & Recreation Clubs
- **Experience & Inclusions**: Private members' residential clubs with Olympic-sized swimming pools, tennis and squash courts, wellness spas, multi-cuisine dining, and private banquet facilities.

### The Lodhi (New Delhi)
- **Location**: Lodhi Road, Lutyens' Delhi
- **Facility Type**: Iconic 5-Star Luxury Hotel
- **Experience & Inclusions**: Member of The Leading Hotels of the World, luxury hotel with private plunge pools in rooms, tranquil gardens, award-winning restaurants, and premier spa.
