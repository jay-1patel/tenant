## 3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the booking amount to reserve an apartment?
A. You can reserve any available 2 BHK or 3 BHK apartment with an initial booking token of ₹1,00,000 (One Lakh Rupees). For 4 BHK luxury residences, the token amount is ₹2,50,000. Booking can be done via net banking (NEFT/RTGS), cheque, or UPI.

### Q2. Is the booking amount refundable if my loan is not approved?
A. Yes. If a buyer's home loan application is rejected by all approved banks due to technical or non-borrower reasons within 21 days of booking, 100% of the token amount is refunded without any cancellation deductions.

### Q3. When will I get possession of the flats?
A. Skyline Elegance Towers A & B are Ready-to-Move with Occupancy Certificate (OC) received. Towers C & D are scheduled for handover by December 2026. Skyline Grandeur luxury residences are scheduled for possession by March 2027.

### Q4. Are car parking spaces included in the cost?
A. Yes, every 2 BHK and 3 BHK apartment comes with 1 covered basement car parking space allocated. 4 BHK penthouses and sky villas include 2 dedicated car parking slots. Additional parking spaces can be purchased subject to availability.

### Q5. Can NRIs (Non-Resident Indians) buy property at Skyline Realty?
A. Yes, absolutely. NRIs and OCIs can purchase residential and commercial properties in India without prior RBI approval. Payments can be routed through NRE/NRO accounts or foreign inward remittances. Our dedicated NRI desk provides end-to-end video tours, digital documentation, and power-of-attorney execution support.

### Q6. Are pets allowed in Skyline communities?
A. Yes, Skyline communities are pet-friendly. We have a dedicated landscaped pet park and pet care guidelines to ensure harmonious community living.

### Q8. What payment plans and schemes do you offer?
A. We offer flexible payment plans including Construction Linked Plans (CLP) tied to slab casting milestones, a limited-period 10:90 Developer Subvention Scheme (pay 10% now and nothing until possession for eligible units), and an exclusive 6% upfront discount for down payment buyers.

### Q9. Which banks have pre-approved home loans for Skyline Realty projects?
A. Our projects are pre-approved by SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Meridian Bank, and Bank of Baroda with attractive interest rates starting from 8.40% p.a. and hassle-free 48-hour approvals. Our in-house Home Loan Desk coordinates documentation and disbursement end-to-end.
