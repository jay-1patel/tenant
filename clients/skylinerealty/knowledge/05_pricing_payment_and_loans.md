# 💳 Pricing, Payment Schemes & Home Loan Assistance

Skyline Realty provides transparent pricing and flexible payment structures to make home buying seamless.

---

## 1. Transparent Pricing Policy
- **No Hidden Charges:** All quotes include basic sale price, clubhouse membership, one-time infrastructure charges, and 1 covered car parking space.
- **Government Taxes & Statutory Dues:**
  - GST: 5% (affordable housing GST of 1% where applicable, standard 5% for residential units).
  - Stamp Duty: 4.9% (Gujarat state rates; female buyers get concession as per state policy).
  - Registration Fees: 1% with sub-registrar.
  - Maintenance Deposit: Advance 2-year maintenance collected at the time of possession.

---

## 2. Flexible Payment Schemes
- **Construction Linked Plan (CLP) — Standard Schedule:**
  - 10% on Booking / Token confirmation.
  - 10% on Agreement for Sale execution (within 30 days).
  - 10% on Completion of Foundation / Plinth.
  - 10% on Casting of 1st Floor Slab.
  - 10% on Casting of 5th Floor Slab.
  - 10% on Casting of 10th Floor Slab.
  - 10% on Casting of Top Floor Slab.
  - 10% on Completion of Brickwork & Internal Plaster.
  - 10% on Completion of Flooring & Sanitaryware installation.
  - 10% on Notice of Possession and Handover.
- **Special 10:90 Developer Subvention Scheme (Limited Period):**
  - Pay 10% now at booking.
  - 90% loan funded by pre-approved banks with EMI sharing option until possession.
- **Down Payment Discount:**
  - Buyers opting for full payment upfront receive an exclusive 6% upfront discount on basic unit cost.
