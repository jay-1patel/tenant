# 🏢 Skyline Realty & Developers — Corporate Profile & Overview

## 1. About Skyline Realty
Skyline Realty is a premier real estate development group with over 18 years of excellence in crafting landmark residential communities, luxury villas, and Grade-A commercial IT parks. 
- Over 25 completed projects spanning 6.5 million square feet.
- More than 4,800 happy families residing in Skyline communities.
- On-time delivery track record with 100% RERA compliance and transparent documentation.

## 2. Core Values & Mission
- **Structural Integrity & Quality Construction:** High-grade Mivan aluminum formwork construction for earthquake-resistant and superior finish structures.
- **Sustainable Architecture:** IGBC Green Certified buildings, incorporating solar rooftops, decentralized wastewater treatment, and 75% lush open green landscaping.
- **Customer First Philosophy:** Zero hidden charges, transparent milestone-based construction payment plans, and complete post-possession maintenance assistance.
