# 🏊 Amenities, Clubhouse & Sustainable Living

Skyline communities provide 360-degree lifestyle infrastructure with over 40+ resort-grade amenities.

---

## 1. The Club Skyline (50,000 sq.ft. Lifestyle Clubhouse)
- **Aquatic Complex:** Temperature-controlled indoor swimming pool, open-air infinity lap pool, and toddler splash pool.
- **Fitness & Wellness:** State-of-the-art gymnasium equipped with Technogym machines, cardio theater, yoga & pilates studio, steam & sauna rooms.
- **Sports & Recreation:**
  - Full-size badminton court (wooden flooring).
  - Squash court & table tennis room.
  - Multi-purpose floodlit turf for box cricket & football.
  - Outdoor tennis court and half basketball court.
- **Community & Leisure:**
  - 40-seater mini-cinema preview theater with Dolby Atmos surround sound.
  - Banquet hall with attached lawn for 300+ guests.
  - Senior citizens lounge, library, and business co-working pods with high-speed Wi-Fi.

---

## 2. Greenery, Landscaping & Kids Zones
- **75% Open Green Landscape:** Designed by renowned landscape architects featuring Japanese Zen gardens, reflexology pathways, and sensory flower gardens.
- **Children's Adventure Arena:** Modern rubberized multi-play station, sand pit, skating rink, and outdoor trampoline.
- **Pet-Friendly Zone:** Dedicated fenced pet park with agility toys and washing station.

---

## 3. Smart Infrastructure & 3-Tier Security
- **3-Tier 24x7 Security:** RFID vehicle boom barriers, biometric visitor entry via MyGate/NoBrokerHood app, 24x7 CCTV monitoring in all common zones.
- **Fire Safety & Disaster Readiness:** Advanced automatic sprinkler systems, fire hydrants on every floor, earthquake-resistant Zone 3 structural design.
- **Eco-Friendly Initiatives:**
  - 100 kW rooftop solar photovoltaic arrays for common area lighting.
  - Rainwater harvesting pits ensuring zero groundwater wastage.
  - On-site Organic Waste Converter (OWC) producing manure for landscaping.
  - Dedicated EV charging stations in basement and visitor parking.
