Seema Darshan
About Seema Darshan
Seema Darshan is a historic frontier outpost on the India‑Pakistan border, offering visitors a
          unique glimpse into the nation’s guard posts, a ritual parade at sunset, and a chance to explore the
          surrounding natural beauty.
Located near the town of Nada Bet, the site blends military heritage with scenic landscapes, featuring a
          watch‑tower, museum, and a vibrant camel show that showcases local culture.
Nada Bet is a unique destination located near the India-Pakistan border in the serene landscape of Gujarat’s
          Banaskantha district. Surrounded by vast stretches of white desert and seasonal wetlands, this remarkable
          place offers visitors an unforgettable glimpse into the life and dedication of the Border Security Force
          (BSF). The famous Seema Darshan program allows travelers to witness patriotic activities, including the
          impressive retreat ceremony performed by BSF jawans during sunset. Visitors can also explore weapon displays,
          military exhibits, and informative galleries showcasing the strength and discipline of India’s border forces.
          Adding to the experience, the traditional camel parade and cultural presentations create a memorable blend of
          patriotism, adventure, and local heritage.
